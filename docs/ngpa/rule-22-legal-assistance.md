# Rule XXII - Legal Assistance

## Sections 106-107

This rule covers the legal assistance available in procurement disputes and the representation of parties.

---

## Section 106. Legal Assistance

### 106.1 Availability of Legal Assistance
Parties to procurement disputes may seek legal assistance from:
- Private counsel of their choice
- Government legal offices (for government entities)
- Appropriate legal aid organizations

### 106.2 Role of Legal Counsel
Legal counsel may:
- Advise clients on procurement laws and regulations
- Prepare legal documents
- Represent clients in proceedings
- File motions, protests, and appeals

### 106.3 Procuring Entity Legal Support
Procuring Entities may seek legal assistance from:
- The Office of the Government Corporate Counsel (for GOCCs)
- The Office of the Solicitor General
- Their internal legal offices
- Other authorized legal counsel

### 106.4 Costs
Legal fees shall be:
- Borne by the party engaging the counsel
- Subject to recovery if awarded by the adjudicating body

---

## Section 107. Representation

### 107.1 Authorized Representatives
Parties may be represented by:
- Duly authorized officers (for corporations/entities)
- Agents with proper authorization
- Legal counsel

### 107.2 Authorization Requirements
Representatives must present:
- Secretary's Certificate or Board Resolution (for corporations)
- Special Power of Attorney (for individuals)
- Proof of authority to appear and bind the principal

### 107.3 Scope of Authority
The authorization shall clearly state:
- The extent of authority granted
- The specific proceedings covered
- Any limitations on the authority

### 107.4 Liability
Representatives shall be:
- Bound by their actions on behalf of their principals
- Subject to administrative, civil, or criminal liability for violations
- Accountable for misrepresentations
