# Rule VIII - Receipt and Opening of Bids

## Sections 52-58

This rule covers eligibility requirements, short-listing, submission, modification, bid security, validity, and opening of bids.

---

## Section 52. Eligibility Requirements for the Procurement of Goods, Infrastructure Projects, and Consulting Services

### 52.1 Basic Eligibility Document
Prospective bidders shall submit their valid and updated **PhilGEPS Certificate of Registration (Platinum Membership)**.

**Additional Technical and Financial Documents:**
- a) Statement of all ongoing government and private contracts
- b) Statement of SLCC (Single Largest Completed Contract) for Goods and Infrastructure
- c) NFCC computation (or committed Line of Credit for Goods)
- d) For Consulting Services: Statement of nationality and confirmation of registered professionals

### 52.2 Joint Venture Requirements
- a) For Goods and Consulting Services: Valid JVA or duly notarized statements from potential JV partners
- b) For Infrastructure Projects: JVA in accordance with RA No. 4566 (Contractors' License Law)

Each partner shall submit their respective PhilGEPS Certificates of Registration.

### 52.3 Foreign Bidder Requirements
All documents must be in English or accompanied by English translation. For Apostille Convention Contracting Parties, documents shall be authenticated through an apostille.

### 52.4 Eligibility Criteria

#### 52.4.1 For Goods

**Eligible Entities:**
- a) Duly licensed Filipino citizens or sole proprietorships
- b) Partnerships (at least 60% Filipino interest)
- c) Corporations (at least 60% Filipino outstanding capital stock)
- d) Cooperatives duly organized under Philippine laws
- e) Joint ventures (at least 60% Filipino ownership)

**Foreign Bidders May Participate When:**
- a) Provided for under any treaty or international/executive agreement
- b) Reciprocal rights are granted by the foreign country
- c) Goods are not available from local suppliers
- d) To prevent situations that defeat competition

**SLCC Requirement:**
- Completed within **ten (10) years** from submission
- Value at least **50%** of ABC (adjusted to current prices)

**NFCC Computation:**
```
NFCC = [(Current assets - current liabilities) × 15] - value of all outstanding contracts
```
NFCC must be at least equal to ABC.

**Line of Credit Option:**
If a committed Line of Credit is submitted, it must be at least **10%** of ABC.

#### 52.4.2 For Infrastructure Projects

**Eligible Entities:**
- Same as Goods, but joint ventures may have less than 60% Filipino ownership (not less than 25%) for structures requiring specialized techniques/technologies

**PCAB License Required:**
Per RA No. 4566 (Contractors' License Law) as amended by RA No. 11711

**SLCC Requirement:**
- Value at least **50%** of ABC
- Shall be supported by Owner's Certificate of Final Acceptance or CPES rating of at least Satisfactory

**NFCC Computation:**
Same formula as Goods, must be at least equal to ABC.

#### 52.4.3 For Consulting Services

**Eligible Entities:**
- Same categories as Goods
- When regulated professions are involved, personnel shall be Filipino citizens and registered professionals

**Foreign Consultants:**
May be hired when Filipino consultants do not have sufficient expertise, as determined by the HoPE.

### 52.5 GOCC Participation
GOCCs may participate if they:
- Are legally and financially autonomous
- Operate under commercial law
- Are not attached agencies of the Procuring Entity

### 52.6 Reassessment of Qualifications
The Procuring Entity can reassess a bidder's qualifications at any stage if there are reasonable grounds to believe a misrepresentation was made or if there has been a change in capability.

## Section 53. Short-Listing for Consulting Services

### 53.1 Short-Listing Process

#### 53.1.1 Consideration
Only those consultants whose contracts are similar in nature and complexity to the procurement project shall be considered.

#### 53.1.2 Number of Short-listed Consultants
- Minimum: **three (3)**
- Maximum: **seven (7)**
- Preferable: **five (5)**

If only one or fewer pass eligibility check and minimum score, the BAC shall consider the same.

#### 53.1.3 Short-listing Criteria
- a) Applicable experience of the consultant
- b) Qualification of personnel who may be assigned
- c) Current workload relative to capacity

### 53.1.4 Timeline
The entire process of eligibility checking and short-listing shall not exceed **twenty (20) calendar days**.

## Section 54. Submission and Receipt of Bids

### 54.1 Two-Envelope System
Bidders shall submit bids in **two (2) separate sealed bid envelopes**:
- First envelope: Technical component (including eligibility requirements)
- Second envelope: Financial component

### 54.2 First Bid Envelope Contents

**For Goods:**
- PhilGEPS Certificate of Registration (Platinum Membership)
- Statement of ongoing contracts
- Statement of SLCC
- NFCC Computation or Line of Credit
- JVA (if applicable)
- Bid security
- Technical Specifications
- Omnibus Sworn Statement
- Certification for foreign bidders claiming reciprocal rights

**For Infrastructure Projects:**
- PhilGEPS Certificate of Registration (Platinum Membership)
- PCAB License and Registration
- Statement of ongoing contracts
- Statement of SLCC
- NFCC Computation
- JVA (if applicable)
- Bid security
- Project Requirements (organizational chart, personnel list, equipment list)
- Omnibus Sworn Statement

**For Consulting Services:**
- Bid security
- Organizational chart
- List of completed and ongoing projects
- Approach, work plan, and schedule
- List of key personnel
- Omnibus Sworn Statement
- PhilGEPS Certificate of Registration (Platinum Membership)

### 54.3 Omnibus Sworn Statement
The Omnibus Sworn Statement shall contain certifications regarding:
- Authorization of signatory
- Not blacklisted
- Authenticity of documents
- Authorization to verify documents
- Compliance with disclosure provisions
- Compliance with labor laws
- Compliance with bidder responsibilities
- No commission or fee given to government representatives
- Advance payment conditions

### 54.4 Second Bid Envelope
Shall contain the financial documents as specified in the PBDs.

### 54.5 Submission Periods

| Category | Maximum Period from Last Day of Posting |
|----------|----------------------------------------|
| Goods | 45 calendar days |
| Infrastructure (≤₱50M) | 50 calendar days |
| Infrastructure (>₱50M) | 65 calendar days |
| Consulting Services | 75 calendar days |

### 54.6 Late Bids
Bids submitted after the deadline shall not be accepted. BAC shall record the bidder's name, representative, and time of late submission.

### 54.7 Notification
The BAC Secretariat shall notify all bidders in writing within **seven (7) calendar days** from the date of bid opening.

### 54.8-54.9 Bid Requirements
- Original copy shall be typewritten or written in ink
- Unsealed or unmarked envelopes shall be rejected
- Improperly sealed envelopes may be accepted if bidder acknowledges the condition

## Section 55. Modification and Withdrawal of Bids

### 55.1 Modification
Bidders may modify their bids before the deadline. The original bid shall not be retrieved; a modification shall be submitted separately, marked as "modification."

### 55.2 Withdrawal
Bidders may withdraw their bids by letter before the deadline. Withdrawal after the deadline is subject to appropriate sanctions. Bidders who withdraw shall not be allowed to submit another bid for the same contract.

---

## Section 56. Bid Security

### 56.1 Requirement
All bids in competitive modes of procurement shall be accompanied by a bid security.

**Purpose:** Guarantee that the successful bidder shall, within **ten (10) calendar days** from receipt of the Notice of Award, enter into contract and furnish performance security.

### 56.2 Forms and Amounts

| Form of Bid Security | Amount (Not less than) |
|---------------------|------------------------|
| Cash, cashier's/manager's check | 2% of ABC |
| Bank draft/guarantee, irrevocable Letter of Credit | 5% of ABC |
| Surety bond | 5% of ABC |

### 56.3 Acceptable Forms
Procuring Entity shall indicate at least **three (3) acceptable forms**, including Bid Securing Declaration.

### 56.4 Currency
Bid security shall be denominated in Philippine Peso.

### 56.5 Return of Bid Security
Returned after the winning bidder has signed the contract and furnished the performance security.

### 56.6 Bid Securing Declaration
An undertaking that the bidder shall enter into contract within **ten (10) calendar days** from receipt of Notice of Award. Violation results in automatic disqualification and blacklisting.

### 56.7 Validity
Bid security shall not be returned later than the expiration of the bid validity period.

---

## Section 57. Bid Validity

### 57.1 Maximum Period
Bids and bid securities shall be valid for a reasonable period, not exceeding **one hundred twenty (120) calendar days** from the date of opening of bids.

### 57.2 Extension
The Procuring Entity may request extension beyond 120 days. Bidders have the right to refuse without forfeiting their bid security.

---

## Section 58. Bid Opening

### 58.1 Timing
The BAC shall open bids immediately after the deadline for submission and receipt.

### 58.2 Opening Manner

| Award Criterion | Opening Procedure |
|----------------|-------------------|
| LCRB | Technical and financial proposals opened on the same day |
| MEARB or MARB | Only technical proposals opened initially; financial proposals of bidders meeting minimum technical score opened later |

### 58.3 Electronic Submission
Passwords for accessing Bidding Documents will be disclosed by Bidders only during the actual bid opening.

### 58.4 Postponement
If bids cannot be opened as scheduled, the BAC shall secure the bids and reschedule on the next working day or soonest possible time through a Notice of Postponement.

### 58.5 Transparency
- Bidders or their representatives may attend the opening
- Abstract of bids and minutes shall be made available to the public upon written request and payment of specified fee
