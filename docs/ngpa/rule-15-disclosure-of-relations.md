# Rule XV - Disclosure of Relations

## Sections 81-82

This rule covers the disclosure of relations between bidders and officials involved in procurement.

---

## Section 81. Disclosure of Relations

### 81.1 Mandatory Disclosure
All bidders and contractors are required to make a **full disclosure of their relations**, where applicable, with the following persons or entities:

- a) Members of the HoPE's immediate family
- b) Members of the BAC
- c) Members of the TWG
- d) The BAC Secretariat
- e) Any officials and employees with authority over the procurement process
- f) End-user/s or its personnel

### 81.2 Scope of Disclosure
The disclosure shall include:
- Direct or indirect pecuniary interest
- Business relationships
- Employment relationships
- Family relationships within the third degree of consanguinity or affinity

### 81.3 Form of Disclosure
The disclosure must be:
- Made under oath
- Submitted together with the bid
- In the form prescribed by the GPPB

### 81.4 Effect of Non-Disclosure
**Failure to disclose** relations as required shall:
- Be a ground for disqualification of the bid
- Subject the bidder to administrative, civil, and/or criminal liability

---

## Section 82. Effects of Disclosure

### 82.1 Evaluation of Disclosed Relations
The BAC shall evaluate all disclosed relations to determine:
- Whether there is a conflict of interest
- Whether the bidder should be disqualified
- Whether any official should be inhibited from the procurement process

### 82.2 Conflict of Interest
A conflict of interest exists when:

- a) A bidder has a direct or indirect pecuniary interest in the procurement
- b) Any member of the BAC, TWG, Secretariat, or other involved official:
  - Has a financial interest in the bidder
  - Is related within the third degree of consanguinity or affinity to the bidder or its officers
  - Has a business or employment relationship with the bidder

### 82.3 Actions Upon Finding Conflict of Interest
When a conflict of interest is found:

- a) If involving a bidder: The bid shall be disqualified
- b) If involving a BAC member or other official:
  - The concerned official shall inhibit from the procurement process
  - A replacement shall be designated as necessary
  - The procurement process shall continue

### 82.4 Documentation
All disclosures and actions taken pursuant to this rule shall be:
- Properly documented
- Kept as part of the procurement records
- Subject to audit and review
