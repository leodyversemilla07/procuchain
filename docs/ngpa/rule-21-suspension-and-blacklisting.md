# Rule XXI - Suspension and Blacklisting

## Sections 98-105

This rule covers the suspension and blacklisting of bidders and contractors for procurement violations.

---

## Section 98. Suspension

### 98.1 Authority to Suspend
The HoPE may **suspend** a bidder/contractor from participating in procurement activities.

### 98.2 Grounds for Suspension
Suspension may be imposed when:

- a) The bidder/contractor is under investigation for procurement violations
- b) There is prima facie evidence of fraud, collusion, or violation
- c) The bidder/contractor has pending administrative or criminal case
- d) To prevent further damage to the government during investigation

### 98.3 Duration of Suspension
- Suspension shall be for a period of **not more than ninety (90) calendar days**
- May be extended if the investigation is ongoing
- Shall automatically be lifted if no case is filed within the suspension period

### 98.4 Effects of Suspension
During the suspension period:
- The bidder/contractor shall not be allowed to participate in any bidding
- Ongoing contracts shall continue unless otherwise ordered
- The suspension shall be posted in PhilGEPS

---

## Section 99. Blacklisting

### 99.1 Definition
**Blacklisting** is the disqualification of a bidder/contractor from participating in government procurement activities for a specified period.

### 99.2 Authority to Blacklist
The following are authorized to impose blacklisting:
- The HoPE (for the Procuring Entity only)
- The GPPB (for all government procurement nationwide)

---

## Section 100. Grounds for Blacklisting

### 100.1 Mandatory Grounds
The following shall be grounds for **automatic blacklisting**:

- a) Submission of falsified eligibility documents
- b) Conviction for fraud, corruption, or other crimes involving moral turpitude
- c) Collusion or bid-rigging
- d) Abandonment of contract without justifiable cause
- e) Failure to complete the contract despite imposition of liquidated damages
- f) Acts constituting corrupt practices under RA 3019

### 100.2 Discretionary Grounds
The following may be grounds for blacklisting:

- a) Withdrawal of bid after bid opening without valid cause
- b) Refusal to accept an award without valid cause
- c) Failure to post performance security
- d) Sub-contracting without authority
- e) Repeated violations of contract terms
- f) Other analogous acts

---

## Section 101. Duration of Blacklisting

### 101.1 Period of Blacklisting

**For Mandatory Grounds:**
- First offense: **Two (2) years**
- Second offense: **Perpetual disqualification**

**For Discretionary Grounds:**
- First offense: **One (1) year**
- Second offense: **Two (2) years**
- Third offense: **Perpetual disqualification**

### 101.2 Computation
The period shall be computed from the date of finality of the blacklisting decision.

---

## Section 102. Procedure for Blacklisting

### 102.1 Investigation
Upon discovery of a ground for blacklisting:
- The BAC shall conduct a preliminary investigation
- The bidder/contractor shall be notified in writing
- An opportunity to explain shall be given

### 102.2 Notice and Hearing
The bidder/contractor shall be given:
- Written notice of the charges
- At least **seven (7) calendar days** to submit a written explanation
- Opportunity to present evidence

### 102.3 Resolution
The BAC shall submit its recommendation to the HoPE within **fifteen (15) calendar days** from:
- Receipt of the explanation
- Or expiration of the period to submit explanation

### 102.4 Decision
The HoPE shall decide within **seven (7) calendar days** from receipt of the BAC recommendation.

---

## Section 103. Effects of Blacklisting

### 103.1 Disqualification
A blacklisted bidder/contractor shall be:
- Disqualified from participating in any procurement activity
- Ineligible to enter into any contract with the government
- Posted in PhilGEPS blacklist

### 103.2 Ongoing Contracts
- Existing contracts shall be terminated
- The Procuring Entity shall take over the works/deliverables
- Securities shall be forfeited

### 103.3 Related Entities
The blacklisting shall extend to:
- Owners, stockholders, officers, and directors
- Related companies under common ownership or management
- Successor entities

---

## Section 104. Appeals

### 104.1 Motion for Reconsideration
The blacklisted party may file a motion for reconsideration within **seven (7) calendar days** from receipt of the decision.

### 104.2 Appeal to GPPB
If the motion is denied, the party may appeal to the GPPB within **fifteen (15) calendar days** from receipt of the denial.

### 104.3 Judicial Review
Decisions of the GPPB may be appealed to the courts in accordance with the Rules of Court.

---

## Section 105. Registry of Blacklisted Bidders/Contractors

### 105.1 Establishment
The GPPB shall establish and maintain a **Registry of Blacklisted Bidders/Contractors**.

### 105.2 Contents
The registry shall contain:
- Name of the blacklisted entity
- Owners, stockholders, officers, and directors
- Ground for blacklisting
- Date and duration of blacklisting
- Procuring Entity that imposed the blacklisting

### 105.3 Publication
The registry shall be:
- Published in PhilGEPS
- Accessible to all Procuring Entities
- Updated regularly

### 105.4 Duty to Verify
Procuring Entities shall:
- Verify if a bidder is blacklisted before accepting any bid
- Use the PhilGEPS blacklist as reference
- Report any violations to the GPPB
