# Rule V - Bids and Awards Committee

## Sections 41-46

This rule covers the composition, functions, and operations of the Bids and Awards Committee (BAC).

---

## Section 41. The BAC and its Composition

### 41.1 BAC Structure

#### 41.1.1 Establishment
The Procuring Entity shall establish in its head office a BAC to undertake the functions specified in this IRR.

#### 41.1.2 Multiple BACs
To expedite the procurement process, the HoPE may create BACs where the number and complexity of the items to be procured shall so warrant.

### 41.2 BAC Composition

#### 41.2.1 Membership
The HoPE shall designate **five (5) or seven (7)** permanent officials of unquestionable integrity and procurement proficiency as members of the BAC.

#### 41.2.2 Composition for NGAs, GOCCs, GFIs, SUCs

**Regular Members:**
- a) Chairperson - at least a 3rd ranking permanent official
- b) An officer - at least a 5th ranking permanent official (legal or administrative area)
- c) An officer - at least a 5th ranking permanent official (finance area)

**Provisional Members:**
- a) An officer with technical expertise relevant to the procurement
- b) A representative from the end-user or implementing unit

The Chairperson and Vice-Chairperson shall be designated by the HoPE. The Vice-Chairperson shall be a regular member.

#### 41.2.3 Composition for LGUs

**For Provinces, Cities, and Municipalities:**
- Representatives from regular offices under the Office of the Local Chief Executive
- A representative from the end user unit

**For Barangays:**
- BAC shall be composed of at least 5 or 7 regular members of the Sangguniang Barangay
- Punong Barangay shall designate the Chairperson, Vice-Chairperson, and members

**For Sangguniang Kabataan:**
- BAC shall be composed of 5 or 7 regular members of the SK
- SK Chairperson shall designate the Chairperson, Vice-Chairperson, and members

#### 41.2.4 Alternate Members
The HoPE may designate alternate BAC members with the same qualifications as original members.

#### 41.2.5 Restrictions
In no case shall the HoPE or the approving authority be a member of the BAC. The Chief Accountant and Local Treasurer cannot be a regular member.

#### 41.2.6 Term of Office
Members shall have a fixed term of **one (1) year**, renewable at the discretion of the HoPE. No member shall serve for more than **three (3) consecutive terms**, except when allowed by the HoPE.

## Section 42. Functions of the BAC

### 42.1 BAC Functions
The BAC shall have the following functions:

- a) Recommend use of any mode of procurement to the HoPE
- b) Publish or post the Invitation to Bid or Request for Expressions of Interest
- c) Conduct pre-procurement and pre-bid conferences
- d) Determine the eligibility of prospective bidders
- e) Receive and open bids
- f) Conduct the evaluation of bids
- g) Undertake post-qualification proceedings
- h) Resolve requests for reconsideration
- i) Recommend award of contracts to the HoPE
- j) Recommend the imposition of sanctions
- k) Prepare a procurement monitoring report
- l) Perform other related functions, including creation of TWG

### 42.2 Quorum
A majority of the total BAC composition shall constitute a quorum, provided the presence of the Chairperson or Vice-Chairperson is required.

### 42.3 Meetings
The Chairperson or Vice-Chairperson shall preside at all meetings. Meetings may be conducted electronically. Decision of at least a majority of those present shall be valid and binding.

## Section 43. Observers

### 43.1 Observer Composition
The BAC shall invite, in addition to the COA representative, at least **two (2) observers** with no voting rights:

#### 43.1.1 Private Group Representative
- For Infrastructure Projects: national associations of constructors (PCAI, NCAP, Inc.)
- For Goods: Philippine Chamber of Commerce and Industry
- For Consulting Services: project-related professional organization (PRC or Supreme Court accredited)

#### 43.1.2 CSO/PO Representative
The other observer shall come from a civil society organization (CSO) or people's organization (PO).

**Qualifications:**
- a) Knowledge, experience or expertise in procurement
- b) Absence of actual or potential conflict of interest
- c) Other relevant criteria determined by the GPPB

### 43.2 Invitation Timeline
Observers shall be invited at least **five (5) calendar days** before the date of the procurement stage or activity. Absence of observers will not nullify BAC proceedings if duly invited.

### 43.4 Responsibilities of Observers
- a) Prepare reports covering observations of proceedings
- b) Submit reports to HoPE, PhilGEPS, and COA within **seven (7) calendar days** after each procurement activity
- c) Immediately inhibit if there is any actual or potential interest in the procurement project

### 43.5 Document Access
Observers shall be allowed to access:
- Minutes of relevant BAC meetings
- Abstract of Bids
- Post-qualification summary report
- APP and related PPMP
- Opened proposals

All observers shall enter into a confidentiality agreement.

## Section 44. Procurement Unit

### 44.1 Establishment
There shall be a procurement unit, division, or office in each Procuring Entity which shall serve as Secretariat to the BAC.

### 44.2 Ad Hoc Secretariat
Pending creation of a procurement unit, an ad hoc BAC Secretariat may be designated by the HoPE.

### 44.3 Secretariat Functions
- a) Provide administrative support to the BAC and TWG
- b) Organize and arrange BAC and TWG meetings and conferences
- c) Prepare minutes of meetings and resolutions
- d) Take custody of procurement documents and records
- e) Manage sale and distribution of Bidding Documents
- f) Publish or post bidding opportunities and Notices of Awards
- g) Assist in managing procurement processes
- h) Monitor procurement activities and milestones
- i) Consolidate PPMPs and prepare the APP
- j) Act as central channel of communications for the BAC

### 44.4 Head of Secretariat Qualifications
- Central offices: at least 5th ranking permanent employee
- Bureaus, regional, sub-regional, district offices: at least 3rd ranking permanent employee

---

## Section 45. Honoraria

The Procuring Entity may grant payment of honoraria to all members of the BAC, TWG, and ad hoc Secretariat in an amount not exceeding **thirty percent (30%)** of their respective basic monthly salaries.

**Note:** Officials and employees whose organic functions are primarily related to procurement are not entitled to honoraria.

---

## Section 46. Professionalization of Government Procurement Practitioners

### 46.1 Creation of Positions
The DBM shall create procurement positions in the government based on the qualification standards recommended by the GPPB and approved by the CSC.

### 46.2 GPPB Responsibilities
The GPPB shall develop:
- a) A competency framework
- b) A certification framework
- c) A code of ethics for public procurement professionals
- d) An inclusive capacity development program

### 46.3 Professional Licensing
The PRC, in consultation with the GPPB, shall establish, administer, and regulate a professional licensing program and continuing education of public procurement professionals.
