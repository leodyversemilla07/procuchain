# Rule VII - Invitation to Bid

## Sections 49-51

This rule covers pre-procurement conference, publication requirements, and pre-bid conference procedures.

---

## Section 49. Pre-Procurement Conference

### 49.1 Mandatory vs. Optional
Prior to the publication of the Invitation to Bid or Request for Expression of Interest, the BAC is mandated to hold a pre-procurement conference on each and every procurement.

**Optional for:**
- Goods: ₱5,000,000 and below
- Infrastructure Projects: ₱10,000,000 and below
- Consulting Services: ₱2,000,000 and below

**Attendees:**
- BAC
- TWG
- Secretariat
- End-User or Implementing Unit
- Consultants hired by the Procuring Entity

**Conference Activities:**
- a) Confirm description, scope, ABC, and contract duration
- b) Ensure procurement is in accordance with PPMP and APP
- c) Determine readiness of procurement:
  - Availability of appropriations
  - Completeness of Bidding Documents
  - Completion of detailed engineering (for Infrastructure Projects)
  - Availability of right-of-way site/location
- d) Review, modify and agree on criteria for eligibility screening, evaluation, and post-qualification
- e) Review and adopt procurement schedule, deadlines, and timeframes
- f) Emphasize confidentiality measures and agree on sanctions

### 49.2 Award Criteria Determination
During the pre-procurement conference, the BAC shall resolve whether to use:

| Criterion | Application | Description |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| **LCRB** | Goods & Infrastructure | Eligibility, technical responsiveness, and lowest financial bid |
| **MEARB** | Goods & Infrastructure | Eligibility, technical responsiveness, and most economically advantageous bid (quality-price ratio) |
| **MARB** | Goods & Infrastructure | Eligibility, technical responsiveness, and highest rated offer based on quality component |
| **HRRB** | Consulting Services | Eligibility, qualifications, technical responsiveness, and highest rated offer |

## Section 50. Publication and Contents of the Invitation to Bid

### 50.1 Publication Requirements
All invitations to bid shall be published continuously by the Procuring Entity.

#### 50.1.1 Publication Channels
- Website or social media platforms of the Procuring Entity
- Other channels authorized by the GPPB

#### 50.1.2 Alternative
For justifiable reasons, Procuring Entities that cannot publish online shall publish in a newspaper of general nationwide circulation at least once.

### 50.2 Contents of the Invitation to Bid or Request for Expression of Interest

The Invitation shall provide the following information:

- a) Name and description of procurement project
- b) Identification and number of lots or items
- c) General statement on criteria for eligibility check, short-listing, evaluation, post-qualification, and award
- d) Whether contract will be awarded to LCRB, MEARB, MARB, or HRRB
- e) Criteria and weights for MEARB or HRRB evaluation
- f) Criteria and weights for MARB evaluation
- g) Evaluation procedure type for Consulting Services
- h) Date, time, and place of deadlines and conferences
- i) ABC for the project, lot, or item
- j) Source of funding
- k) Where Bidding Documents may be secured/downloaded
- l) Contract duration or delivery schedule
- m) Contact information of the Procuring Entity
- n) Bid security requirement
- o) Other necessary information
- p) Electronic bid submission provisions (if applicable)

### 50.3 Publication Periods

#### 50.3.1 For Competitive Bidding, Competitive Dialogue, and Unsolicited Offer with Bid Matching

**Seven (7) calendar days** posting requirement at:
- Conspicuous place in premises
- PhilGEPS website
- Procuring Entity website (if available)
- Foreign government/financing institution website (if applicable)

#### 50.3.2 For Other Modes

**Three (3) calendar days** minimum posting at:
- PhilGEPS website
- Procuring Entity website (if available)
- Conspicuous place in premises

**Applicable to:**
- a) Limited Source Bidding
- b) Two Failed Biddings
- c) Small Value Procurement (ABC above ₱200,000)
- d) NGO Participation

#### 50.3.3 Electronic Bid Submission Certification
If the Procuring Entity will allow electronic bid submission, the highest IT official shall issue a Certification based on the GPPB-prescribed form, submitted to GPPB-TSO prior to posting.

## Section 51. Pre-bid Conference

### 51.1 When Required
For procurement projects with an ABC of **₱3,000,000 or more**, the BAC shall conduct at least one (1) pre-bid conference to clarify requirements, terms, conditions, and specifications.

**Optional for:** ABC less than ₱3,000,000 (at BAC discretion)

### 51.2 Timing
The pre-bid conference shall be held:
- At least **twelve (12) calendar days** before the deadline for submission and receipt of bids
- Not earlier than **seven (7) calendar days** from PhilGEPS posting of the Invitation to Bid or Bidding Documents

### 51.3 Conference Activities

The pre-bid conference shall discuss:
- Eligibility requirements
- Technical and financial components
- Questions and clarifications

**BAC's Proactive Role:**
- a) Discuss requirements and conduct walk-through presentations
- b) Discuss common reasons for bidders' disqualifications from previous projects

**Format:** May be conducted in person, face-to-face, or online through videoconferencing, webcasting, or similar technology.

**Attendance:** Open to prospective bidders but not mandatory.

### 51.4 Documentation
- Proceedings shall be recorded
- Minutes shall be prepared not later than **five (5) calendar days** after the pre-bid conference
- Available to prospective bidders within **five (5) calendar days** upon written request
- Decisions amending Bidding Documents shall be issued through a **Supplemental Bid Bulletin** at least **seven (7) calendar days** before the deadline

### 51.5 Bid Bulletins

#### 51.5.1 Requests for Clarification
- Must be in writing
- Submitted at least **ten (10) calendar days** before the deadline
- BAC shall respond through Bid Bulletin at least **seven (7) calendar days** before deadline

#### 51.5.2 Initiative-Based Bulletins
For clarifying or modifying Bidding Documents, the Procuring Entity may issue Bid Bulletins at least **seven (7) calendar days** before deadline. Any modification shall be identified as an amendment.

#### 51.5.3 Posting Requirements
Bid Bulletins shall be posted in:
- PhilGEPS
- Conspicuous place in premises
- Website or social media of the Procuring Entity
- Other authorized channels

Bidders who have submitted bids before issuance of the Bid Bulletin must be informed and allowed to modify or withdraw their bids.
