# Rule XIX - The Government Procurement Policy Board (GPPB)

## Section 91

This rule establishes the composition, powers, and functions of the Government Procurement Policy Board.

---

## Section 91. The Government Procurement Policy Board

### 91.1 Creation and Purpose
The **Government Procurement Policy Board (GPPB)** is created to:
- Formulate public procurement policies
- Issue rules, regulations, and guidelines
- Coordinate government procurement activities
- Ensure the implementation of the NGPA

### 91.2 Composition
The GPPB shall be composed of:

**Chairperson:**
- Secretary of the Department of Budget and Management (DBM)

**Co-Chairperson:**
- Director-General of the National Economic and Development Authority (NEDA)

**Members:**
- Secretary of the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI)
- Secretary of the Department of Science and Technology (DOST)
- Secretary of the Department of Finance (DOF)
- Secretary of the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH)
- One (1) representative from the private sector

### 91.3 Powers and Functions
The GPPB shall have the following powers and functions:

- a) **Policy Formulation:** Formulate and recommend policies and rules governing public procurement
- b) **Rule-Making:** Issue rules, regulations, and guidelines to implement the NGPA
- c) **Standard Forms:** Prescribe standard forms for procurement documents
- d) **Monitoring:** Monitor the implementation of the NGPA and this IRR
- e) **Capacity Building:** Conduct training programs on public procurement
- f) **Information System:** Establish and maintain an integrated procurement information system
- g) **Resolution of Issues:** Resolve procedural issues and provide clarification on procurement matters
- h) **Review and Update:** Periodically review and update procurement rules and regulations
- i) **International Agreements:** Coordinate procurement matters under international treaties or agreements
- j) **Annual Report:** Submit an annual report to the President and Congress

### 91.4 The GPPB-Technical Support Office (GPPB-TSO)
The GPPB shall be supported by a **Technical Support Office** which shall:

- a) Provide technical and administrative support to the GPPB
- b) Conduct research and studies on procurement
- c) Manage the Philippine Government Electronic Procurement System (PhilGEPS)
- d) Implement training and capacity building programs
- e) Monitor and evaluate procurement processes
- f) Prepare policy recommendations for GPPB consideration

### 91.5 Issuances
The GPPB may issue:
- Circulars
- Memoranda
- Guidelines
- Resolutions
- Other issuances to implement the NGPA

### 91.6 Meetings
- The GPPB shall meet at least once a month
- Special meetings may be called as necessary
- A quorum shall consist of a majority of the members

### 91.7 Secretariat
The GPPB shall have a secretariat to:
- Prepare meeting agendas
- Document proceedings
- Follow up on GPPB decisions
- Coordinate with member agencies
