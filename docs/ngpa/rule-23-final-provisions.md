# Rule XXIII - Final Provisions

## Sections 108-117

This rule contains the final provisions of the NGPA IRR including effectivity, repealing clause, and transitory provisions.

---

## Section 108. Separability Clause

If any provision of this IRR is declared invalid or unconstitutional, the remaining provisions not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and effect.

---

## Section 109. Repealing Clause

### 109.1 Repealed Provisions
All laws, decrees, executive orders, rules and regulations, and other issuances or parts thereof which are inconsistent with the provisions of the NGPA and this IRR are hereby **repealed or modified** accordingly.

### 109.2 Specific Repeals
The following are specifically repealed:
- Previous IRR of RA 9184
- Inconsistent GPPB issuances
- Other conflicting rules and regulations

---

## Section 110. Amendments

### 110.1 Authority to Amend
This IRR may be amended by the GPPB in accordance with the provisions of the NGPA.

### 110.2 Procedure for Amendment
Amendments shall:
- Be subject to public consultation
- Be published in the Official Gazette or newspaper of general circulation
- Take effect fifteen (15) days after publication

---

## Section 111. Supplementary Rules

### 111.1 Authority to Issue
The GPPB may issue supplementary rules, regulations, and guidelines to implement this IRR.

### 111.2 Coverage
Supplementary rules may cover:
- Specific types of procurement
- Particular sectors or industries
- Emerging issues and technologies
- Procedural clarifications

---

## Section 112. Training and Capacity Building

### 112.1 Mandatory Training
Procurement officers and BAC members shall undergo mandatory training on:
- The NGPA and its IRR
- Procurement procedures and best practices
- Ethics in procurement
- PhilGEPS operations

### 112.2 Certification
The GPPB may require certification for procurement practitioners.

---

## Section 113. Monitoring and Evaluation

### 113.1 GPPB Monitoring
The GPPB shall monitor the implementation of this IRR through:
- Regular reporting by Procuring Entities
- Audit findings
- Complaints and feedback
- Statistical data from PhilGEPS

### 113.2 Evaluation
Periodic evaluation shall be conducted to:
- Assess the effectiveness of procurement policies
- Identify areas for improvement
- Recommend policy reforms

---

## Section 114. Annual Procurement Report

### 114.1 Submission
All Procuring Entities shall submit an **Annual Procurement Report** to the GPPB.

### 114.2 Contents
The report shall include:
- Summary of procurement activities
- Total procurement expenditure
- Modes of procurement used
- Number of failed biddings
- Procurement performance indicators
- Issues and recommendations

### 114.3 Deadline
The report shall be submitted within **ninety (90) calendar days** after the end of each fiscal year.

---

## Section 115. Transitory Provisions

### 115.1 Pending Procurements
Procurement activities that have commenced before the effectivity of this IRR shall continue to be governed by the rules and regulations in effect at the time of the commencement thereof.

### 115.2 Commencement Defined
Procurement activities shall be deemed to have commenced upon:
- Publication of the Invitation to Bid
- Issuance of the Request for Expression of Interest
- Sending of the Request for Quotation

### 115.3 Ongoing Contracts
Contracts executed before the effectivity of this IRR shall:
- Continue to be governed by the terms and conditions thereof
- Be subject to amendments only in accordance with this IRR

### 115.4 Transition Period
A transition period of **one (1) year** from the effectivity of this IRR is provided for:
- Compliance with new requirements
- Updating of systems and processes
- Training of personnel

---

## Section 116. Publication

This IRR shall be published in:
- The Official Gazette
- At least two (2) newspapers of general circulation
- The PhilGEPS website

---

## Section 117. Effectivity

This IRR shall take effect **fifteen (15) days** after its complete publication in:
- The Official Gazette, or
- A newspaper of general circulation

---

## Acknowledgment

This document represents the Implementing Rules and Regulations of Republic Act No. 9184, as amended by Republic Act No. 12009 and related laws, collectively known as the **New Government Procurement Act (NGPA)**.

The NGPA establishes the framework for government procurement in the Philippines, promoting:
- **Transparency** in procurement processes
- **Competitiveness** through fair bidding
- **Accountability** of public officials
- **Efficiency** in the use of public funds
- **Modernization** through electronic procurement
