# Rule XVI - Protest Mechanism

## Sections 83-87

This rule establishes the protest mechanism for addressing complaints and disputes in procurement proceedings.

---

## Section 83. Protest on Decisions of the BAC

### 83.1 Right to Protest
Any bidder may file a **motion for reconsideration** and/or **protest** against decisions of the BAC, including but not limited to:

- a) Declaration of eligibility or ineligibility
- b) Post-disqualification
- c) Declaration of the Lowest/Highest Calculated Bid
- d) Post-qualification
- e) Declaration of the Lowest/Highest Calculated Responsive Bid
- f) Award of contract

### 83.2 Form of Protest
Protests must be:
- In writing
- Under oath
- Stating the grounds relied upon

### 83.3 Filing Requirements
The protest shall:
- Be filed within the reglementary period
- Be accompanied by the required protest fee
- Contain all relevant documentary evidence

---

## Section 84. Filing of a Motion for Reconsideration

### 84.1 Reglementary Period for Motion for Reconsideration
A motion for reconsideration must be filed within **three (3) calendar days** from receipt of the written notice or upon verbal notification.

### 84.2 Contents of Motion for Reconsideration
The motion shall:
- Clearly state the grounds for reconsideration
- Present new evidence, if any
- Be verified under oath

### 84.3 Resolution of Motion for Reconsideration
The BAC shall resolve the motion for reconsideration within **seven (7) calendar days** from receipt thereof.

### 84.4 Effect of Filing
The filing of a motion for reconsideration shall:
- Stay the procurement proceedings
- Suspend the running of periods for subsequent actions

---

## Section 85. Protest Fee

### 85.1 Amount of Protest Fee
The protest fee shall be equivalent to:
- **One percent (1%)** of the ABC
- Or a minimum of **Fifty Thousand Pesos (PhP 50,000.00)**, whichever is higher

### 85.2 Payment of Fee
The fee shall be:
- Paid in cash
- Deposited in escrow with the Procuring Entity
- Non-refundable if the protest is found to be without merit

### 85.3 Refund of Fee
If the protest is found to be meritorious:
- The protest fee shall be refunded to the protesting bidder
- Within fifteen (15) calendar days from resolution

---

## Section 86. Filing of a Protest or Request for Reconsideration to the Head of the Procuring Entity

### 86.1 Appeal to HoPE
A bidder may file a **verified position paper** with the HoPE within **seven (7) calendar days** from:
- Receipt of the BAC's resolution denying the motion for reconsideration
- Failure of the BAC to resolve the motion within the prescribed period

### 86.2 Contents of Position Paper
The position paper shall contain:
- A summary of facts
- Issues to be resolved
- Arguments and applicable laws/regulations
- Supporting documentary evidence

### 86.3 Resolution by HoPE
The HoPE shall decide on the protest within **seven (7) calendar days** from receipt of the position paper.

### 86.4 Effect of HoPE Decision
The decision of the HoPE shall:
- Be final and binding on the Procuring Entity
- Be immediately executory
- Not preclude further legal remedies

---

## Section 87. Effect of Protest on Procurement Proceedings

### 87.1 Suspension of Proceedings
**As a general rule:**
Filing of a protest shall NOT suspend the procurement proceedings.

### 87.2 Exceptions
The HoPE may suspend procurement proceedings if:
- There is prima facie evidence of irregularity
- Suspension is necessary to protect the interest of the Government
- The protest raises substantive issues requiring thorough investigation

### 87.3 Continuation of Proceedings
If no suspension is ordered:
- The Procuring Entity shall continue with the procurement process
- The protesting party may still pursue legal remedies

### 87.4 Effect of Favorable Resolution
If the protest is resolved in favor of the protesting bidder:
- The Procuring Entity shall take appropriate corrective action
- This may include re-evaluation, re-bidding, or award to the protesting bidder

### 87.5 Documentation
All protests and resolutions shall be:
- Properly documented
- Part of the procurement records
- Available for audit review
