# Rule XII - Sustainable Public Procurement

## Sections 72-77

This rule covers sustainable public procurement, green public procurement strategy, and inclusive procurement programs.

---

## Section 72. Sustainable Public Procurement

### 72.1 SPP Program Requirement
All Procuring Entities shall establish and implement a Sustainable Public Procurement (SPP) Program aimed at achieving value for money on a whole life cycle basis.

**Objectives:**
- Meet specific needs of government agencies
- Create broader societal and economic benefits
- Reduce adverse environmental impacts

### 72.2 Integration of Sustainability
Procuring Entities must integrate sustainability considerations from procurement planning, budgeting to implementation, contract management, and disposal.

#### 72.2.1 Environmental Aspect
Goods, services, and infrastructure that minimize negative impacts must be prioritized.

#### 72.2.2 Social Aspect
Procuring Entities must consider:
- Fair labor practices
- Respect human rights
- Promote gender equity, inclusivity
- Support MSMEs, social enterprises, cooperatives, and other marginalized sectors

### 72.3 GPPB Guidelines
The GPPB will issue guidelines on necessary sustainability criteria.

## Section 73. Green Public Procurement Strategy

### 73.1 Definition and Implementation
Green Public Procurement (GPP) is an approach whereby Procuring Entities seek to procure Goods, Infrastructure Projects, and Consulting Services with reduced environmental impact through their life cycle.

#### 73.1.1 Environmental Sustainability Integration
Should be integrated from the outset, encompassing:
- Project identification
- Design
- Budget preparation

**Evaluation Weight:** Sustainability of products, materials, or structures with green specifications shall be given greater weight in the evaluation of Bids.

### 73.2 Green Criteria in Planning and Design

**Criteria to Apply:**
- Energy efficiency
- Sustainable materials
- Waste reduction

**Green Specifications to Focus On:**
- Energy-efficient products
- Renewable or recyclable materials
- Reduced greenhouse or carbon emissions and pollutants

#### 73.2.1 Life Cycle Integration
Procuring Entities must incorporate environmental considerations throughout the project life cycle.

#### 73.2.2 Standards Alignment
Specifications must align with environmental standards and certifications.

#### 73.2.3 Environmental Criteria Assessment

**For Goods:**
- Technical considerations for each item's environmental impact
- Apply Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) per Section 13

**For Infrastructure Projects:**
- Integrate Environmental Impact Statement (EIA) requirement
- Comply with Presidential Decree No. 1096 (Philippine Green Building Code)

#### 73.2.4 Market Scoping for GPP

Priority areas for market scoping include:
- a) Range of potential bidders for green solutions
- b) Current level of green business practices
- c) Potential for innovative or emerging technologies
- d) Influence of public spending
- e) Market motivations and challenges
- f) Standards and tools required
- g) Collaboration opportunities

### 73.3 GPP Objectives

Public procurement will prioritize items and services that enhance:
- Energy, water, and material efficiency
- Waste reduction
- Pollution and emissions prevention

**Goals:**
- Reduce climate change
- Promote local and rural development
- Enhance sustainability of supply chains and infrastructure
- Boost industrial productivity, innovation, and competitiveness
- Encourage inclusive business models and green jobs
- Support transition to circular economy

### 73.4 Monitoring and Reporting
Procuring Entities must monitor and report on GPP Strategy implementation regularly, including yearly reports to the GPPB indicating:
- Quantity of procurements with environmental criteria
- Value for money achieved

### 73.5 GPPB Implementation
The GPPB shall ensure incorporation and implementation of sustainability and GPP concepts.

**Phased Implementation:** Taking into account readiness of government agencies and green product suppliers.

The GPPB may prescribe standard bidding documents, forms, and evaluation documents reflecting sustainability principles.

## Section 74. Development of a Green Local Market

### 74.1 Validation and Certification Program
The DTI, in consultation with relevant government agencies and the private sector, shall develop a cost-efficient program for:
- Validation and certification of green products
- Attracting green investments
- Enhancing competition within government procurement

#### 74.1.1 Program Contents
- Clear criteria for identifying green products
- Compliance with recognized environmental standards
- Efficient and transparent certification process

### 74.2 MSME Accessibility
The program shall be designed to reduce financial and administrative burden on businesses, especially MSMEs, by ensuring the certification process is:
- Accessible
- Affordable
- Aligned with international best practices

### 74.3 Regular Review and Update
The DTI shall regularly review and update certification criteria to reflect:
- Advancements in environmental standards
- Technologies
- Market trends

The DTI shall also maintain an up-to-date directory of green local vendors.

## Section 75. Inclusive Procurement Program

### 75.1 Equal Opportunities
Procuring Entities shall encourage active participation of registered sectors by giving weight to:
- Gender parity
- Ethnic equity
- Other social factors

**Integration Focus:**
- Marginalized groups
- Socially responsible enterprises

**Goals:**
- Contribute to poverty reduction
- Respect fundamental labor standards

### 75.2 Strategic Planning Integration

#### 75.2.1 Accessibility and Inclusivity
Procuring Entities should consider accessibility and inclusivity when identifying requirements.

#### 75.2.2 Social Impact Evaluation
Evaluate social impacts by applying Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) per Section 13.

#### 75.2.3 Market Scoping
Activities may include:
- Organizing supplier days
- Training for inclusive suppliers to submit unsolicited Bids
- Active promotion

#### 75.2.4 Fit-for-Purpose Measures
- a) Reserving a percentage of procurement opportunities for inclusive suppliers
- b) Streamlining bidding processes to reduce administrative burdens
- c) Conducting Small Value Procurements to pilot inclusive procurements

### 75.3 Social Criteria in Bidding Documents
Social criteria may include:
- a) Adherence to fundamental labor standards and human rights
- b) Promotion of local job creation or employment opportunities for vulnerable groups
- c) Provision of apprenticeship and training opportunities
- d) Other social and community benefits

### 75.4 Monitoring and Evaluation
Procuring Entities are required to conduct routine monitoring and evaluation of inclusive procurement program results.

### 75.5 Advanced Notice
Procuring Entities shall identify inclusive procurement projects in their indicative APP to give advanced notice to the market.

### 75.6 Yearly Reports
Reports shall include:
- Participation of vulnerable sectors
- Breakdown of contracts awarded
- Gender parity attained
- Compliance with social responsibility standards

### 75.7 Gender-Responsive Procurement Guidelines
The GPPB will issue guidelines prioritizing women-led or women-owned businesses by ensuring their meaningful and active participation in public procurement.

## Section 76. Registration of Certain Sectors

### 76.1 Registry of Sectors
The GPPB shall maintain a registry of entities belonging to:

| Sector | Certifying Agency |
|--------|-------------------|
| Farmers | Department of Agriculture (DA) |
| Fisherfolk | Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources (BFAR) |
| Persons with disabilities | National Council for Disability Affairs (NCDA) |
| Solo parents | Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD) |
| Microenterprises and social enterprises | MSMED Council |
| Startups, spin-offs | DTI, DICT, NIC, or DOST |
| Cooperatives | CDA |
| Other relevant sectors | As determined by GPPB |

---

## Section 77. Exemptions and other Forms of Support

The GPPB, after consultation with relevant agencies (DA, BFAR, NCDA, DSWD, MSMED Council, DTI, DICT, NIC, DOST, CHED, CDA), shall prescribe:
- Instances for outright exemption
- Other forms of support for the registered sectors

Relevant guidelines may be issued based on data gathered from Section 76.
