# Rule XVIII - Contract Prices and Warranties

## Sections 89-90

This rule covers contract price adjustments and warranty provisions.

---

## Section 89. Contract Price

### 89.1 Fixed Price Contracts
Government contracts shall, as a general rule, be on a **fixed price basis**.

### 89.2 Price Adjustment
Price adjustment may be allowed in the following cases:

- a) **Extraordinary increase in costs** of construction materials
- b) **Changes in the scope of work** ordered by the Procuring Entity
- c) **Adjustments due to government-mandated increases** in costs (e.g., minimum wage)
- d) **Fluctuation in foreign currency rates** for contracts with foreign currency components
- e) Other circumstances as provided in the contract or by the GPPB

### 89.3 Price Escalation/De-escalation Formula
For price adjustment:
- The contract shall specify the formula for computing price escalation/de-escalation
- The formula shall be based on official indices (e.g., PSA, BSP)
- Both escalation and de-escalation shall be applied

### 89.4 Limitations on Price Adjustment
- Price adjustment shall not exceed the **ABC**
- The total adjusted price shall not exceed the original contract price plus **twenty percent (20%)** unless approved by the appropriate authority
- Price adjustment shall be supported by appropriate documentation

### 89.5 Approval of Price Adjustment
Price adjustments shall be approved by:
- The HoPE for adjustments up to ten percent (10%) of the original contract price
- Higher authorities as prescribed by existing laws and regulations for higher amounts

## Section 90. Warranty

### 90.1 Warranty Security
For the procurement of goods, the Procuring Entity shall require:
- A **warranty security** in the form of cash, bank guarantee, letter of credit, or surety bond
- The security shall be equivalent to a **percentage of the contract price**

### 90.2 Warranty Period

**For Goods:**
- One (1) year warranty from date of acceptance
- Or as specified in the contract

**For Infrastructure Projects:**
- **Contractor's All Risk Insurance (CARI)** during construction
- **One (1) year warranty** from date of acceptance (defects liability period)
- **Fifteen (15) year structural warranty** from date of acceptance for buildings
- **Ten (10) year warranty** for other civil/structural works from acceptance

**For Consulting Services:**
- As specified in the contract

### 90.3 Warranty Coverage
The warranty shall cover:
- Defective materials
- Faulty workmanship
- Non-conformance to specifications
- Manufacturing defects
- Hidden defects

### 90.4 Warranty Obligations
During the warranty period:

- a) The supplier/contractor shall repair, replace, or correct defects **at no cost** to the Procuring Entity
- b) The Procuring Entity shall notify the supplier/contractor of any defect
- c) The supplier/contractor shall respond within the time specified in the contract
- d) If the supplier/contractor fails to respond, the Procuring Entity may:
  - Undertake corrective action at the supplier's/contractor's expense
  - Forfeit the warranty security
  - Institute administrative, civil, or criminal action

### 90.5 Extension of Warranty
The warranty period may be extended:
- For the duration of repairs
- As specified in the contract
- By mutual agreement of the parties

### 90.6 Release of Warranty Security
The warranty security shall be released:
- After the expiration of the warranty period
- Upon verification that all warranty obligations have been fulfilled
- Within fifteen (15) calendar days from expiration of the warranty period

### 90.7 Conditions for Warranty Claims
For valid warranty claims:
- The defect must not be due to normal wear and tear
- The goods/works must have been used/operated according to specifications
- The claim must be made within the warranty period
- Proper documentation must be maintained
