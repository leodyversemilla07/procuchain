# Required Documents Per Procurement Stage
## Philippine Municipal-Level Procurement (RA 9184 & RA 12009)

**Date**: November 15, 2025  
**Based on**: RA 9184 (Government Procurement Reform Act), RA 12009 (New Government Procurement Act - NGPA), and IRR  
**Scope**: Municipal/LGU Level Procurement Process  
**Document Status**: COMPLETED & SIGNED documents only

---

## Document Upload Requirements

**IMPORTANT**: All documents uploaded to the system must be:
- ✅ **COMPLETED** - Fully filled out with all required information
- ✅ **SIGNED** - Bearing all necessary signatures from authorized signatories
- ✅ **APPROVED** - With proper approvals where required
- ✅ **DATED** - Showing date of signing/approval
- ✅ **SCANNED** - Clear, legible PDF copies of original documents

---

## PHASE 1: PRE-PROCUREMENT (Planning & Preparation)

### Stage 1: Procurement Initiation
**Purpose**: Formally initiate procurement with approved requirements and budget

#### Required Documents (ALL must be completed & signed):

1. **Purchase Request (PR)** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Signed by: End-user/Requesting Office Head
   - Approved by: Budget Officer, Accountant, Treasurer
   - Must include: Item specifications, quantities, estimated costs

2. **Project Procurement Management Plan (PPMP)** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Signed by: End-user/Department Head
   - Approved by: Planning Officer, Budget Officer
   - Must include: Annual procurement requirements, timeline

3. **Annual Procurement Plan (APP)** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Signed by: Budget Officer, Municipal Accountant
   - Approved by: Mayor/Municipal Administrator (HoPE)
   - Must include: All planned procurements for the fiscal year

4. **Certificate of Availability of Funds (CAF)** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Signed by: Municipal Budget Officer
   - Certified by: Municipal Accountant
   - Must show: Available budget allocation

5. **Approved Budget for the Contract (ABC)** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Signed by: Budget Officer
   - Approved by: Mayor/Municipal Administrator
   - Must include: Detailed cost breakdown

6. **Technical Specifications/Terms of Reference (TOR)** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Prepared by: Technical Working Group (TWG)
   - Signed by: End-user, TWG Head
   - Must include: Detailed technical requirements

7. **Market Research/Price Quotations** (if applicable)
   - Compiled by: BAC Secretariat/End-user
   - Must include: At least 3 price canvass from suppliers

8. **Sangguniang Bayan Resolution** (if required)
   - For contracts requiring legislative approval
   - Signed by: Presiding Officer, Members present
   - Must include: Resolution number, date of approval

9. **Environmental Compliance Certificate (ECC)** (if applicable)
   - For infrastructure projects
   - Issued by: DENR
   - Must be valid and current

10. **Program of Work (POW)** (for infrastructure projects)
    - Prepared by: Municipal Engineer/TWG
    - Signed by: Municipal Engineer
    - Must include: Detailed work breakdown

---

### Stage 2: Pre-Procurement Conference
**Purpose**: Initial discussion of procurement requirements with stakeholders

#### Required Documents (ALL must be completed & signed):

1. **Pre-Procurement Conference Agenda** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Prepared by: BAC Secretariat
   - Signed by: BAC Chairman
   - Must include: Date, time, venue, topics

2. **Attendance Sheet** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Signed by: All attendees (BAC members, TWG, end-users)
   - Must include: Full names, positions, signatures

3. **Minutes of Pre-Procurement Conference** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Prepared by: BAC Secretariat
   - Signed by: BAC Chairman, BAC Members present
   - Attested by: BAC Secretary
   - Must include: Discussion points, decisions, action items

4. **Presentation Materials** (if used)
   - Prepared by: End-user/TWG
   - Must include: Project overview, requirements, timeline

5. **Questions and Clarifications Log**
   - Prepared by: BAC Secretariat
   - Must include: All questions raised and responses given

---

### Stage 3: Bidding Documents
**Purpose**: Prepare complete bidding documents for publication

#### Required Documents (ALL must be completed & signed):

1. **Invitation to Bid (ITB)** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Signed by: BAC Chairman
   - Must include: Project title, ABC, schedule of activities

2. **Bid Data Sheet (BDS)** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Prepared by: BAC Secretariat/TWG
   - Signed by: BAC Chairman
   - Must include: Project-specific information, deadlines

3. **Instructions to Bidders** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Prepared by: BAC Secretariat (using standard template)
   - Signed by: BAC Chairman
   - Must include: Eligibility requirements, bid submission rules

4. **General Conditions of Contract (GCC)** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Using standard government GCC template
   - Signed by: BAC Chairman

5. **Special Conditions of Contract (SCC)** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Prepared by: TWG
   - Signed by: BAC Chairman
   - Must include: Project-specific terms and conditions

6. **Technical Specifications** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Prepared by: TWG/End-user
   - Signed by: Technical Officer, BAC Chairman
   - Must be detailed and non-restrictive

7. **Bill of Quantities (BOQ)** (for infrastructure/goods)
   - Prepared by: Municipal Engineer/TWG
   - Signed by: Municipal Engineer, BAC Chairman
   - Must include: Complete itemized list with quantities

8. **Drawings/Plans** (for infrastructure projects)
   - Prepared by: Municipal Engineer/Licensed Architect
   - Signed by: Licensed Professional (with PRC license)
   - Stamped: Professional seal
   - Must be complete and to scale

9. **Forms and Formats** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Bid Form
   - Price Schedule
   - Omnibus Sworn Statement
   - Bid Securing Declaration
   - All signed by: BAC Chairman

10. **BAC Resolution Approving Bidding Documents** ✅ REQUIRED
    - Signed by: All BAC Members
    - Attested by: BAC Secretary
    - Must include: Resolution number, date

11. **PhilGEPS Posting Receipt** ✅ REQUIRED
    - Screenshot/confirmation of posting
    - Must show: Date and time of posting

12. **Newspaper Advertisement** (if ABC exceeds threshold)
    - Affidavit of Publication
    - Clipping of published advertisement
    - Must show: Date and newspaper name

13. **Website Posting Proof**
    - Screenshot of LGU website posting
    - Must be visible and accessible

---

## PHASE 2: PROCUREMENT (Bidding & Evaluation)

### Stage 4: Pre-Bid Conference
**Purpose**: Clarify bidding requirements and answer prospective bidders' questions

#### Required Documents (ALL must be completed & signed):

1. **Pre-Bid Conference Agenda** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Prepared by: BAC Secretariat
   - Signed by: BAC Chairman
   - Must include: Date, time, venue

2. **Attendance Sheet** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Signed by: All attendees (BAC members, prospective bidders, observers)
   - Must include: Company name, representative name, contact details

3. **Minutes of Pre-Bid Conference** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Prepared by: BAC Secretariat
   - Signed by: BAC Chairman, BAC Members present
   - Attested by: BAC Secretary
   - Must include: All questions and official BAC responses

4. **Sign-in Sheet for Bidders**
   - Signed by: All prospective bidders
   - Must include: Company details, authorized representative

5. **Audio/Video Recording** (recommended)
   - Transcript if available
   - For dispute resolution purposes

6. **PowerPoint Presentation** (if used)
   - Prepared by: BAC/TWG
   - Must be shared with all attendees

---

### Stage 5: Supplemental Bid Bulletin
**Purpose**: Issue clarifications, modifications, or amendments to bidding documents

#### Required Documents (ALL must be completed & signed):

1. **Supplemental/Bid Bulletin** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Prepared by: BAC Secretariat
   - Signed by: BAC Chairman, all BAC Members
   - Must include: Bulletin number, date, specific changes

2. **BAC Resolution Approving Bid Bulletin** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Signed by: All BAC Members
   - Attested by: BAC Secretary
   - Must include: Justification for amendments

3. **PhilGEPS Posting Receipt** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Screenshot/confirmation of bulletin posting
   - Must show: Date and time of posting

4. **Notice to All Bidders Who Purchased Documents** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Proof of notification (email, letters, SMS)
   - Must include: Date and time of notification

5. **Website Posting Proof**
   - Screenshot of updated posting on LGU website

6. **Acknowledgment Receipts from Bidders** (if hand-delivered)
   - Signed by: Each bidder's representative
   - Must show: Date and time received

---

### Stage 6: Bid Opening
**Purpose**: Public opening of sealed bids

#### Required Documents (ALL must be completed & signed):

1. **Bid Submission Register** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Signed by: BAC Secretariat staff receiving bids
   - Must include: Date/time received, bidder name, receipt number

2. **Sealed Bid Envelopes** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Technical Proposal (sealed)
   - Financial Proposal (sealed)
   - Both must be: Intact, properly sealed, addressed to BAC

3. **Abstract of Bids** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Prepared during: Bid opening
   - Signed by: BAC Chairman, all BAC Members present, bidders' representatives
   - Must include: All bid prices, completeness check

4. **Minutes of Bid Opening** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Prepared by: BAC Secretariat
   - Signed by: BAC Chairman, all BAC Members present
   - Attested by: BAC Secretary
   - Must include: Attendance, bids received, bid prices read

5. **Attendance Sheet** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Signed by: BAC members, bidders, observers
   - Must include: All attendees' details

6. **Bidders' Eligibility Documents** (received)
   - PhilGEPS Certificate of Registration
   - Mayor's/Business Permit
   - Tax Clearance Certificate
   - Financial Statements
   - All other eligibility documents per ITB

7. **Bidders' Technical Proposals** (received)
   - Signed Bid Forms
   - Technical specifications compliance
   - Methodology/Work Plan
   - Personnel/Equipment

8. **Bidders' Financial Proposals** (received)
   - Bid prices
   - Price schedules
   - Signed and sealed

9. **Bid Security** (for each bidder)
   - Bid Securing Declaration, OR
   - Cash/Cashier's Check/Bank Draft, OR
   - Bank Guarantee, OR
   - Surety Bond

10. **Audio/Video Recording of Bid Opening** (recommended)
    - Complete recording of proceedings
    - For transparency and dispute resolution

---

### Stage 7: Bid Evaluation
**Purpose**: Technical and financial evaluation of responsive bids

#### Required Documents (ALL must be completed & signed):

1. **BAC Resolution Creating Technical Working Group (TWG)** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Signed by: All BAC Members
   - Attested by: BAC Secretary
   - Must include: TWG members, mandate

2. **Preliminary Examination Report** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Prepared by: BAC Secretariat
   - Signed by: BAC Secretariat Head
   - Must show: Completeness check of all bids

3. **Detailed Technical Evaluation Report** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Prepared by: Technical Working Group (TWG)
   - Signed by: All TWG Members
   - Reviewed by: BAC Chairman
   - Must include: Compliance rating for each bidder

4. **Financial Evaluation Report** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Prepared by: BAC Secretariat
   - Signed by: BAC Members
   - Must include: Calculation of bid prices, adjustments

5. **Comparative Bid Analysis** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Prepared by: BAC Secretariat
   - Signed by: BAC Chairman
   - Must show: Ranking of bidders from lowest to highest

6. **Minutes of BAC Evaluation Meetings** ✅ REQUIRED
   - For each meeting held
   - Signed by: BAC Members present
   - Attested by: BAC Secretary

7. **Supporting Evaluation Documents**
   - Evaluation matrices/scoresheets
   - Technical calculations
   - Compliance checklists

8. **Notices to Bidders** (if clarifications needed)
   - Requests for clarification
   - Bidders' responses
   - All signed and dated

9. **BAC Resolution on Bid Evaluation Results** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Signed by: All BAC Members
   - Must identify: Lowest Calculated and Responsive Bid (LCRB)

---

### Stage 8: Post-Qualification
**Purpose**: Verify eligibility and capacity of lowest calculated bidder

#### Required Documents (ALL must be completed & signed):

1. **Post-Qualification Report** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Prepared by: BAC Secretariat/TWG
   - Signed by: BAC Chairman, all BAC Members
   - Must include: Verification results, recommendation

2. **Site Visit Report** (for infrastructure/goods) ✅ REQUIRED
   - Prepared by: TWG/BAC Secretariat
   - Signed by: Team members who conducted visit
   - Must include: Photos, observations, findings

3. **Document Verification Checklist** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Prepared by: BAC Secretariat
   - Signed by: BAC Secretariat Head
   - Must verify: Authenticity of submitted documents

4. **Financial Capacity Assessment** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Prepared by: Municipal Accountant/TWG
   - Signed by: Accountant/Financial Analyst
   - Must analyze: Financial statements, credit-worthiness

5. **Technical Capacity Assessment** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Prepared by: TWG
   - Signed by: TWG members
   - Must verify: Personnel qualifications, equipment, experience

6. **Certification from Relevant Agencies** (as applicable)
   - DTI Registration
   - SEC Registration
   - CDA Registration (for cooperatives)
   - PhilGEPS Certificate validity confirmation
   - All must be verified as authentic

7. **Site Visit Photos/Videos**
   - With date stamps
   - Showing: Facilities, equipment, personnel

8. **Interview Notes** (if interviews conducted)
   - With: Key personnel
   - Signed by: Interviewers

9. **BAC Resolution on Post-Qualification** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Signed by: All BAC Members
   - Must state: Pass/Fail, with justification

10. **Notice to Lowest Bidder of Post-Qualification Result**
    - If failed: with reasons
    - If passed: proceed to award
    - Signed by: BAC Chairman

---

### Stage 9: BAC Resolution
**Purpose**: Official BAC recommendation for contract award

#### Required Documents (ALL must be completed & signed):

1. **BAC Resolution Recommending Award** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Signed by: All BAC Members (unanimous or with dissent noted)
   - Attested by: BAC Secretary
   - Must include: Resolution number, date, recommended awardee, contract amount

2. **Notice of Lowest Calculated and Responsive Bid (LCRB)** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Prepared by: BAC Secretariat
   - Signed by: BAC Chairman
   - Posted on: PhilGEPS, LGU website, bulletin board

3. **Complete Bid Evaluation Documentation Package** ✅ REQUIRED
   - All evaluation reports
   - All BAC resolutions
   - Meeting minutes
   - Bound and indexed

4. **Transmittal Letter to Head of Procuring Entity (HoPE)** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Signed by: BAC Chairman
   - Addressed to: Mayor/Municipal Administrator
   - Attaching: All supporting documents

5. **PhilGEPS Posting of Award Recommendation** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Screenshot/confirmation
   - For 7-day posting period
   - Must show: Date of posting

6. **Posting Proof on LGU Website**
   - Screenshot of posting
   - Must be accessible to public

7. **Certificate of Posting on Conspicuous Place**
   - Signed by: BAC Secretariat
   - Photo of physical posting

---

## PHASE 3: POST-PROCUREMENT (Award & Implementation)

### Stage 10: Notice of Award
**Purpose**: Official notification of contract award to winning bidder

#### Required Documents (ALL must be completed & signed):

1. **HoPE Approval of BAC Recommendation** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Signed by: Mayor/Municipal Administrator
   - Date: Within 3 calendar days from receipt
   - Must state: Approval or disapproval with reasons

2. **Notice of Award (NOA)** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Prepared by: BAC Secretariat
   - Signed by: Mayor/Municipal Administrator (HoPE)
   - Issued to: Winning bidder
   - Must include: Contract amount, terms, requirements

3. **Certificate of Award Receipt by Winning Bidder** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Signed by: Winning bidder's authorized representative
   - Dated: Showing date of receipt

4. **Publication of Notice of Award** ✅ REQUIRED
   - PhilGEPS posting confirmation
   - LGU website posting screenshot
   - Physical posting proof

5. **Notice to Unsuccessful Bidders** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Individual letters to each unsuccessful bidder
   - Signed by: BAC Chairman
   - Must state: Reason for non-award

6. **Return of Bid Securities** (for unsuccessful bidders)
   - Acknowledgment receipts
   - Signed by: Each unsuccessful bidder

7. **Certificate from Municipal Legal Officer** (if requested)
   - Certifying: Compliance with legal requirements
   - Signed by: Municipal Legal Officer

---

### Stage 11: Performance Bond, Contract and PO
**Purpose**: Execute contract and secure performance bond

#### Required Documents (ALL must be completed & signed):

1. **Performance Security/Bond** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Amount: As specified in bidding documents (usually 10% of contract price)
   - Forms acceptable:
     - Cash
     - Cashier's/Manager's Check
     - Bank Draft/Guarantee
     - Surety Bond from GSIS or accredited insurance company
   - Validity: Throughout contract duration + warranty period
   - Signed by: Supplier/Contractor and Surety Company (if bond)

2. **Contract Agreement** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Prepared by: Municipal Legal Officer/BAC Secretariat
   - Signed by: Mayor/Municipal Administrator (for LGU), Winning Bidder
   - Witnessed by: Municipal Legal Officer, BAC Chairman
   - Notarized: By Notary Public
   - Must include: Scope, price, timeline, terms, all annexes

3. **Contract Annexes** (attached to contract)
   - Bidding Documents
   - Winning Bid
   - Technical Specifications
   - Bill of Quantities
   - General & Special Conditions
   - All referenced documents

4. **Purchase Order (PO)** ✅ REQUIRED (for goods/supplies)
   - Signed by: Municipal Accountant, Treasurer, Mayor
   - Issued to: Winning supplier
   - Must include: Item specifications, quantities, prices, delivery terms

5. **Job Order** (for services)
   - Signed by: Mayor/Department Head
   - Must include: Scope of work, deliverables, timeline

6. **Sangguniang Bayan Resolution** ✅ REQUIRED (if threshold requires)
   - For contracts exceeding prescribed limits
   - Signed by: Presiding Officer, Majority of Sangguniang Members
   - Must include: Approval of contract execution

7. **Certificate of Availability of Funds** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Signed by: Municipal Budget Officer
   - Certified by: Municipal Accountant
   - For contract amount

8. **Obligation Request/Allotment** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Signed by: Budget Officer, Accountant
   - Must obligate: Contract funds

9. **Contractor's/Supplier's Business Documents**
   - Updated PhilGEPS Certificate
   - Valid Mayor's/Business Permit
   - Current Tax Clearance
   - All verified as current

10. **Insurance Policies** (if required)
    - Comprehensive General Liability
    - Workmen's Compensation (for construction)
    - All Risk Insurance
    - All valid and current

11. **Contractor's All Risk (CAR) Insurance** (for construction)
    - Covering: Contract amount
    - Period: Contract duration
    - Naming: LGU as co-assured

12. **Warranty Security** (if required)
    - For goods with warranty period
    - Amount and form: As specified in contract

13. **Receipt of Contract Documents by Contractor** ✅ REQUIRED
    - Signed by: Contractor's authorized representative
    - Acknowledging: Receipt of signed contract and all annexes

---

### Stage 12: Notice to Proceed
**Purpose**: Authorize contractor/supplier to begin work/delivery

#### Required Documents (ALL must be completed & signed):

1. **Notice to Proceed (NTP)** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Signed by: Mayor/Municipal Administrator
   - Issued to: Contractor/Supplier
   - Must include: Commencement date, completion date, reporting requirements

2. **Acknowledgment of NTP Receipt** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Signed by: Contractor/Supplier's authorized representative
   - Dated: Showing date of receipt
   - Contract time starts: As per terms

3. **Construction/Delivery Schedule** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Prepared by: Contractor/Supplier
   - Approved by: Municipal Engineer/Project Manager
   - Must include: Detailed timeline, milestones

4. **List of Key Personnel** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Submitted by: Contractor/Supplier
   - Must include: Names, qualifications, PRC licenses (where required)
   - Signed by: Contractor and Project Manager

5. **Equipment List** (for construction projects)
   - Submitted by: Contractor
   - Must show: Equipment to be used, with proof of ownership/lease

6. **Site Mobilization Report** (for infrastructure)
   - Prepared by: Contractor
   - Approved by: Municipal Engineer
   - Photos of: Equipment, materials at site

7. **Pre-Construction Meeting Minutes** (for infrastructure) ✅ REQUIRED
   - Signed by: All parties (LGU, Contractor, Engineer, Barangay officials)
   - Must discuss: Work schedule, traffic management, safety measures

8. **Barangay Endorsement/Clearance** (if required)
   - For projects within barangay
   - Signed by: Barangay Chairman

9. **Building/Construction Permit** (for infrastructure)
   - Issued by: Municipal Engineer's Office
   - Must be valid throughout project

10. **Health and Safety Plan** (for construction)
    - Prepared by: Contractor's Safety Officer
    - Approved by: Municipal Engineer
    - Must include: Safety procedures, emergency protocols

---

### Stage 13: Monitoring
**Purpose**: Monitor implementation and track progress

#### Required Documents (ALL must be completed & signed):

1. **Progress Reports** ✅ REQUIRED (Weekly/Monthly)
   - Prepared by: Contractor/Supplier
   - Reviewed by: Project Manager/Municipal Engineer
   - Signed by: Both parties
   - Must include: Work accomplished, issues, next steps

2. **Monitoring Reports** ✅ REQUIRED (Regular intervals)
   - Prepared by: Project Manager/End-user
   - Signed by: Department Head, Municipal Engineer (if infra)
   - Submitted to: Mayor, BAC Secretariat
   - Must include: Status, accomplishment %, observations

3. **Site Inspection Reports** ✅ REQUIRED (Regular)
   - Prepared by: Inspection Team
   - Signed by: Municipal Engineer, End-user, QA Officer
   - Must include: Photos, measurements, compliance check

4. **Delivery Receipts** (for goods/supplies)
   - For each delivery
   - Signed by: Property Officer, End-user
   - Must show: Date, quantity, condition

5. **Inspection and Acceptance Report (IAR)** (for goods)
   - Prepared by: Inspection Committee
   - Signed by: Committee members, Property Officer
   - Must verify: Quantity, quality, specifications compliance

6. **Time Extension Request** (if applicable)
   - Submitted by: Contractor/Supplier
   - Must include: Valid reasons, supporting documents
   - Approved by: BAC, Mayor

7. **Variation Order** (if changes needed)
   - Prepared by: Municipal Engineer/TWG
   - Approved by: BAC, Mayor (if cost increase)
   - Signed by: Both parties
   - Must justify: Need for change

8. **Payment Requests/Progress Billings** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Submitted by: Contractor/Supplier
   - Accompanied by: Progress report, photos, delivery receipts
   - Certified by: Municipal Engineer/Project Manager
   - Approved by: Accountant, Budget Officer, Mayor

9. **Disbursement Vouchers** ✅ REQUIRED (for each payment)
   - Prepared by: Accounting Office
   - Signed by: Accountant, Budget Officer, Treasurer, Mayor
   - Supporting documents: Invoice, IAR, progress report

10. **Communication Logs**
    - All correspondence between LGU and Contractor
    - Meeting minutes
    - Email exchanges (printed and filed)

11. **Photos/Videos of Work in Progress**
    - Date-stamped
    - Showing: Progress at different stages
    - Organized by: Date and location

12. **Materials Testing Results** (for construction)
    - Concrete cylinder tests
    - Soil compaction tests
    - Other quality tests
    - Signed by: Accredited testing laboratory

13. **As-Built/As-Delivered Documentation** (ongoing)
    - Updated by: Contractor
    - Shows: Actual implementation vs. plans

---

### Stage 14: Completion
**Purpose**: Final acceptance and closeout of contract

#### Required Documents (ALL must be completed & signed):

1. **Certificate of Completion** ✅ REQUIRED (for infrastructure/services)
   - Issued by: Municipal Engineer/Project Manager
   - Date: Upon satisfactory completion
   - Signed by: Engineer, Contractor, Mayor
   - Must verify: All work completed per contract

2. **Certificate of Final Acceptance** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Issued by: Inspection Committee/End-user
   - Signed by: Committee members, Department Head, Mayor
   - Date: After final inspection
   - Must state: Full compliance with specifications

3. **Final Inspection Report** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Prepared by: Inspection Committee
   - Signed by: All committee members
   - Must include: Photos, test results, punch list resolution

4. **Final Inspection and Acceptance Report (IAR)** ✅ REQUIRED (for goods)
   - Prepared by: Inspection Committee
   - Signed by: Committee members, Property Officer
   - Must verify: Complete delivery, quality

5. **As-Built Plans/Drawings** ✅ REQUIRED (for infrastructure)
   - Prepared by: Contractor's Engineer
   - Signed by: Licensed Professional (with PRC seal)
   - Approved by: Municipal Engineer
   - Must show: Actual construction vs. original plans

6. **Operations and Maintenance Manuals** (for equipment/facilities)
   - Submitted by: Supplier/Contractor
   - Must include: Maintenance procedures, troubleshooting

7. **Warranty Documents** ✅ REQUIRED (if applicable)
   - Warranty Certificate
   - Warranty period
   - Terms and conditions
   - Signed by: Supplier/Manufacturer

8. **Training Completion Certificates** (if training was part of contract)
   - Attendance sheets
   - Training materials provided
   - Certificates issued to participants

9. **Final Progress Report** ✅ REQUIRED
   - Prepared by: Contractor/Supplier
   - Signed by: Project Manager
   - Must summarize: All work completed

10. **Final Payment Request** ✅ REQUIRED
    - Submitted by: Contractor/Supplier
    - Accompanied by: Certificate of Completion, final IAR
    - Must reconcile: All previous payments and balances

11. **Final Billing Statement** ✅ REQUIRED
    - Prepared by: Contractor/Supplier
    - Shows: Total contract price, previous payments, retention, final amount
    - Supported by: Official Invoice, Official Receipt

12. **Clearance/Waiver** ✅ REQUIRED
    - Signed by: Contractor/Supplier
    - Waiving: Future claims related to the project
    - Notarized

13. **Liquidated Damages Assessment** (if delays occurred)
    - Computation of damages
    - Deductions from payment
    - Accepted by: Contractor

14. **Final Disbursement Voucher** ✅ REQUIRED
    - Including: Retention money release (after warranty period for construction)
    - Signed by: Accountant, Budget Officer, Treasurer, Mayor

15. **Turnover Documents** ✅ REQUIRED
    - Turnover Certificate
    - Inventory of items/facilities turned over
    - Signed by: Contractor, End-user, Property Officer, Mayor

16. **Project Completion Report** ✅ REQUIRED
    - Prepared by: Project Manager/BAC Secretariat
    - Signed by: Department Head, BAC Chairman, Mayor
    - Must include:
      - Executive summary
      - Project description
      - Timeline (planned vs. actual)
      - Budget (planned vs. actual)
      - Key achievements
      - Challenges and resolutions
      - Lessons learned
      - Photos before/during/after

17. **Property Acknowledgment Receipt (PAR)** (for goods)
    - Signed by: End-user who will use/keep the items
    - Must show: Item details, serial numbers, location

18. **Certificate of Acceptance and Turnover**
    - For facilities/infrastructure
