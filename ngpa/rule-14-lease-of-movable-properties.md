# Rule XIV - Lease of Movable Properties

## Section 80

This rule covers the lease of movable properties by the government.

---

## Section 80. Lease of Movable Properties

### 80.1 Application of Procurement Rules
The lease of movable properties shall be subject to the provisions of the NGPA and this IRR.

### 80.2 Supplier/Lessor Eligibility
Persons and entities engaged in the lease of movable properties may participate in the bidding for the lease if they can establish their legal, technical, and financial capability to enter the contract.

### 80.3 Evaluation Criteria
The evaluation of bids for the lease of movable properties shall consider:
- Total cost of lease
- Terms and conditions of the lease
- Maintenance and support provisions
- Other relevant factors specified in the bidding documents
