# Rule XI - Award, Implementation and Termination of the Contract

## Sections 66-71

This rule covers the notice of award, contract execution, performance security, and contract implementation/termination.

---

## Section 66. Notice and Execution of Award

### 66.1 BAC Recommendation
The BAC shall recommend to the HoPE the award of contract to the bidder with the LCRB, MEARB, MARB, HRRB, SCRB, SEARB, SARB or SRRB after post-qualification is completed.

**Supporting Documents:**
- a) Resolution of the BAC recommending award
- b) Abstract of Bids
- c) Duly approved program of work or delivery schedule and cost estimates
- d) Document authorizing the Procuring Entity to incur obligations
- e) Other pertinent documents required by existing laws

**Notification:** Within **three (3) calendar days** from issuance of the resolution, the BAC shall notify all other bidders in writing.

### 66.2 HoPE Approval Timeline
Within a period not exceeding **ten (10) calendar days** from the determination and declaration by BAC, the HoPE shall approve or disapprove the recommendation.

### 66.3 Notice of Award
Upon approval, the HoPE shall immediately issue the Notice of Award.

**Disapproval:** Must be based only on valid, reasonable, and justifiable grounds expressed in writing. Copy shall be furnished to the BAC and the bidder.

### 66.4 Subsequent Post-Qualification
When applicable, the BAC shall conduct post-qualification of the bidder with the next LCB, MEAB, or HRB.

**Request for Reconsideration:**
- May be filed within **three (3) calendar days** from receipt of notice of disapproval
- HoPE shall resolve with finality within **seven (7) calendar days**
- Must be resolved before any award is made

### 66.5 Pre-Award Requirements
Within **ten (10) calendar days** from receipt of Notice of Award, the winning bidder shall:

**a) Submit documents:**
- Valid JVA (if applicable)
- Valid PCAB license (for foreign bidders in Infrastructure)
- SEC Certificate of Registration (for foreign corporations/consulting firms)

**b) Post performance security** per Section 68

**c) Sign the contract**

## Section 67. Period of Action on Procurement Activities

### 67.1 Maximum Period
The procurement process, from opening of bids up to award of contract, shall not exceed **sixty (60) calendar days**.

All members of the BAC, Secretariat, TWG, and other relevant personnel shall be on a **"jury duty"** type of assignment until the Notice of Award is issued.

### 67.2 GPPB Authority
The GPPB may adjust the period, but not exceeding **ninety (90) calendar days**.

### 67.3 Deadline Rules
If deadline falls on a non-working day, the deadline shall be the next working day.

**Exception:** For expediency, activities may be held on non-working days if BAC members are available and appropriate bid bulletin is issued.

### 67.4 Tolling/Suspension
The period of action is tolled or suspended:
- a) For EPAs, pending approval of GAA, corporate budget, or appropriations ordinance
- b) During suspension of procurement activities

The period shall begin to run again once the basis for tolling/suspension no longer exists.

## Section 68. Performance Security

### 68.1 Purpose
To guarantee faithful performance by the winning bidder of its obligations under the contract.

The winning bidder must update the performance security for any variation order or amendment to order.

### 68.2 Performance Securing Declaration (PSD)
Sectors under Section 76 may post a PSD in lieu of performance security.

### 68.3 PSD Conditions
The PSD shall state that the winning bidder shall be blacklisted from any government procurement activity for:
- **One (1) year** for first offense
- **Two (2) years** for prior similar offense

### 68.4 Performance Security Schedule

| Form of Performance Security | Amount (Not less than) |
|-----------------------------|------------------------|
| Cash, cashier's/manager's check | Goods/Consulting: 5%, Infrastructure: 10% |
| Bank draft/guarantee, irrevocable Letter of Credit | Goods/Consulting: 5%, Infrastructure: 10% |
| Surety bond | 30% |

### 68.5 Denomination
Performance security shall be denominated in Philippine Peso and posted in favor of the Procuring Entity.

### 68.6 Validity Period
The performance security shall remain effective until the issuance of the Certificate of Final Acceptance.

### 68.7 Additional Performance Security (Infrastructure)
The winning bidder shall post additional security to cover any cumulative increase of more than **10%** over the original contract value due to variation orders.

### 68.8 Proportional Reduction
Reduction is allowed in cases of:
- a) Reduction in contract value
- b) Partially completed works that are usable and accepted

**Conditions:** Reduction must be more than 10% and aggregate reductions not more than 50%.

## Section 69. Failure to Enter into Contract and Post Performance Security

### 69.1 Single Responsive Bid
If the bidder with SCRB, SEARB, SARB, or SRRB fails to:
- Submit required documents
- Enter into contract
- Post required performance security

**Consequences:**
- Disqualification from further participation
- Bid security forfeiture
- Appropriate sanctions
- Failure of bidding declared
- Rebidding undertaken

### 69.2 Multiple Responsive Bids
If the bidder with LCRB, MEARB, MARB, or HRRB fails the same requirements:

**Consequences:**
- Disqualification from further participation
- Bid security forfeiture
- Appropriate sanctions
- BAC shall proceed with post-qualification of the second bidder

---

## Section 70. Reservation Clause

The HoPE reserves the right to reject any and all bids, declare a failure of bidding, or not award the contract in the following situations:

- a) Prima facie evidence of collusion
- b) BAC failed to comply with applicable law or prescribed bidding procedures
- c) Justifiable and reasonable grounds where award will not redound to government benefit:
  - Physical and economic conditions have significantly changed
  - Project is no longer necessary
  - Source of funds has been withheld or reduced

## Section 71. Contract Implementation and Termination

### 71.1 Contract Implementation for Goods

#### 71.1.1 Amendment to Order
May be issued for:
- a) Emergency cases, fortuitous events, or unforeseen contingencies
- b) Contract does not reflect real intention due to mistake or accident
- c) Amendment of delivery schedule based on meritorious grounds
- d) Offered goods no longer available (equivalent or higher specifications accepted)
- e) Other analogous circumstances

**Limitation:** Increase must not exceed **10%** of original contract price; HoPE may approve up to **20%**.

#### 71.1.2 Suspension of Delivery
May be ordered for a specified period, not to exceed original contract duration (unless meritorious reasons exist).

#### 71.1.3 Extension of Contract Time
Shall be submitted before prescribed delivery date. May be approved based on meritorious reasons.

#### 71.1.4 Liquidated Damages
Equal to **1/10 of 1%** of the cost of delayed goods for every day of delay.

**Maximum:** If liquidated damages reach **10%** of total contract price, the Procuring Entity may rescind the contract.

#### 71.1.5 Advance Payment
- Hotel/restaurant services, venue use, office lease: up to **50%**
- Calamity procurement: up to **15%**
- With irrevocable Letter of Credit/bank guarantee: up to **15%** within 60 calendar days

### 71.2 Contract Implementation for Infrastructure Projects

#### 71.2.1 Variation Orders
May cover increase/decrease in quantities or introduction of new work items.

**Limits:**
- Cumulative positive Variation Orders: not exceed **10%** of original contract price
- HoPE may authorize up to **20%** in exceptional cases
- Beyond 20%: subject to another procurement project

#### 71.2.2 Suspension of Work
May be ordered for force majeure, failure to correct unsafe conditions, failure to perform contract provisions, or adjustment of plans.

**Contractor's Right to Suspend:**
- Right-of-way problems
- Requisite construction plans not issued
- Peace and order conditions
- Failure to deliver government-furnished materials
- Delay in payment beyond **45 calendar days**

#### 71.2.3 Extension of Contract Time
Granted when additional work or special circumstances warrant extension. Contractor must notify prior to contract expiration and within **30 calendar days** after the circumstances arose.

#### 71.2.4 Liquidated Damages
Equal to at least **1/10 of 1%** of cost of unperformed portion for every day of delay.

**Maximum:** If liquidated damages reach **10%** of total contract price, the Procuring Entity may rescind or terminate.

#### 71.2.5 Advance Payment
Not exceeding **15%** of total contract price, in lump sum or at most two installments.

**Requirements:**
- Written request of the contractor
- Irrevocable standby Letter of Credit, bank guarantee, or surety bond

#### 71.2.6 Negative Slippage
Procuring Entity shall monitor performance and implement calibrated measures per GPPB guidelines.

#### 71.2.7 Progress Payment
Monthly statement of work accomplished may be submitted. Deductions include:
- Cumulative value previously paid
- Portion of advance payment
- Retention money
- Third-party liabilities
- Amount for uncorrected defects

#### 71.2.8 Retention Money
- **10%** retention until **50%** completion
- No additional retention after 50% if work is satisfactorily done and on schedule
- May be decreased to **5%** based on justifiable causes
- Released upon final acceptance

### 71.3 Contract Implementation for Consulting Services

#### 71.3.1 Contract Amendment
May be made for changes affecting project scope, key personnel, schedule, or contract duration.

**Limit:** Total increase shall not exceed **20%** of contract price.

#### 71.3.2 Suspension of Work
**By Procuring Entity:** If consultant fails to perform obligations.
**By Consultant:** If Procuring Entity fails to deliver furnished equipment, if peace and order conditions prevent work, or if payment is delayed beyond **60 calendar days**.

#### 71.3.3 Extension of Contract Time
Shall consider elapsed time during suspension if not due to consultant's fault.

#### 71.3.4 Liquidated Damages
Equal to **1/10 of 1%** of cost of unperformed portion for every day of delay.

**Maximum:** If liquidated damages reach **10%** of contract amount, the contract may be rescinded or terminated.

### 71.4 Termination of Contracts

#### 71.4.1 Termination for Breach of Contract

**For Goods:** When supplier fails to deliver, amounting to at least **10%** of contract price.

**For Infrastructure Projects:** When any of the following conditions are present:
- Negative slippage of **15%** or more while project is ongoing
- Negative slippage of **10%** or more after contract time has expired
- Abandonment or non-performance without just cause
- Failure to comply with Notice of Rejection within 30 days
- Lack of minimum essential equipment
- Persistent or flagrant neglect
- Neglect to remove defective materials
- Unauthorized subcontracting
- Bankruptcy or insolvency

**For Consulting Services:** When consultant fails to deliver outputs, amounting to at least **10%** of contract price.

#### 71.4.2 Termination due to Force Majeure
When, as a result of force majeure, delivery or performance cannot continue for at least **sixty (60) calendar days**.

#### 71.4.3 Termination for Procuring Entity's Convenience
When conditions make contract implementation economically, financially, or technically impractical or unnecessary.

#### 71.4.4 Termination for Unlawful Acts
When prima facie evidence shows engagement in:
- Corrupt, fraudulent, collusive, and coercive practices
- Using forged documents
- Using adulterated materials or methods
- Any analogous act

#### 71.4.5 Termination by Contractor or Consultant
**Contractor may terminate if:**
- Failure of Procuring Entity to deliver supplies/materials
- Substantial failure of Procuring Entity to perform obligations
- Prolonged suspension
- Disruption due to adverse peace and order situation

**Consultant may terminate if:**
- Procuring Entity is in material breach
- Substantial failure of Procuring Entity to perform
- Prolonged suspension

#### 71.4.6 Procedures for Termination
- a) Verification - within **7 calendar days**
- b) Notice to Terminate
- c) Show Cause - within **7 calendar days** from receipt
- d) Rescission of Notice - before receipt of verified position paper
- e) Decision - within **10 calendar days** from receipt of verified position paper
- f) Contract Termination Review Committee may be created
- g) Take-over of Contracts per Section 35(c)
- h) Options for Termination for Convenience
- i) Notice by Contractor or Consultant - **30 calendar days** before intended termination
