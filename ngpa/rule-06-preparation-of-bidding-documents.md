# Rule VI - Preparation of Bidding Documents

## Sections 47-48

This rule covers the form, content, and access requirements for Bidding Documents.

---

## Section 47. Form and Content of Bidding Documents

### 47.1 Standard Forms and Contents
The Bidding Documents shall be prepared by the Procuring Entity following the standard forms and manuals prescribed by the GPPB. The Bidding Documents shall include the following:

- a) ABC
- b) Invitation to Bid or Request for Expression of Interest
- c) Instructions to Bidders, including:
  - Documents comprising the bid
  - Criteria for eligibility
  - Bid evaluation methodology
  - Post-qualification
  - Weight assigned to each criterion
  - Quality-price ratio if contract to be awarded to MEARB or HRRB
  - Date, time, and place of pre-bid conference, submission, and opening of bids
- d) Technical Specifications, Terms of Reference, or Scope of Work
- e) Eligibility Requirements
- f) Environmental criteria and specifications (per Section 73)
- g) Plans or drawings
- h) Bid Form, Price Schedule, and List of Goods or Bill of Quantities
- i) Delivery Time or Completion Schedule
- j) Form and Amount of Bid Security
- k) Form and Amount of Performance Security and Warranty
- l) Form of Contract, and General and Special Conditions of Contract
- m) Additional documentary requirements or specifications

**Note:** The End-User or Implementing Unit shall be responsible for the preparation of technical specifications, scope of work, or terms of reference.

### 47.2 Clear and Unambiguous Terms
The specifications and other terms shall reflect the necessary specifications required to meet the needs of the Procuring Entity in clear and unambiguous terms.

**Mixed Procurements:** The Procuring Entity shall specify in the Bidding Documents the requirements, criteria, and other conditions applicable to each component of the project.

### 47.3 Availability Period
The BAC shall make the Bidding Documents available from the time the Invitation to Bid or Request for Expression of Interest is first published until the deadline for the submission and receipt of bids.

### 47.4 Bidding Documents Fee

Bidders may be asked to pay a fee to recover the cost for the preparation and development of the Bidding Documents, in accordance with GPPB guidelines.

**Posting Requirements:**
- The BAC shall post the complete Bidding Documents at its website and the PhilGEPS website
- Bidding Documents may be downloaded from any of said websites
- Upon submission of bids, bidders shall pay the applicable fee, if required
- Bidding Documents may also be secured from the BAC Secretariat upon payment

### 47.5 Fee Refund
Bidding Documents fee may be refunded in accordance with GPPB guidelines, based on the grounds provided in Section 70 of the Act and this IRR.

---

## Section 48. Access to Information

### 48.1 Equal Access
In all stages of the preparation of the Bidding Documents, the Procuring Entity shall ensure equal access to information.

**Prior to Official Release:**
No aspect of the Bidding Documents shall be divulged or released to:
- Any prospective bidder
- Person having direct or indirect interest in the project
- Any party

*Exception:* Those officially authorized in the handling of the documents.

### 48.2 Authorized Personnel
Individuals authorized to handle procurement documents include:
- BAC Chairperson
- BAC Members
- BAC Secretariat
- TWG
- Representatives from the End-User or Implementing Unit

### 48.3 National Security Procurements
In procurements involving and affecting national security, the disclosure of specific components of procurement documents (technical specifications, requirements, and components) shall be dependent upon the HoPE, considering:
- Nature of the documents
- Classification
- Sensitivity
- Confidentiality
- Purpose and reason for the request
