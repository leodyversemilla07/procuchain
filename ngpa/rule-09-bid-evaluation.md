# Rule IX - Bid Evaluation

## Sections 59-62

This rule covers the preliminary examination and evaluation of bids for Goods, Infrastructure Projects, and Consulting Services.

---

## Section 59. Preliminary Examination of Bids

### 59.1 First Bid Envelope Examination
The BAC shall open the first bid envelopes in public to determine each bidder's compliance with eligibility and technical requirements.

**Evaluation Method:**
- Non-discretionary "pass or fail" criterion
- Bids that fail to include any requirement or are incomplete/patently insufficient shall be rated "failed"

**Electronic Submission:**
Scanned copies of the first bid envelope are acceptable, subject to submission of originals during post-qualification:
- a) Bid Securing Declaration or other forms of bid security (excluding cash)
- b) Omnibus Sworn Statement

Non-submission within prescribed period shall result in post-disqualification.

### 59.2 Second Bid Envelope Examination
Immediately after determining compliance with the first envelope, the BAC shall open the second bid envelope of each remaining eligible bidder.

**Failure Criteria:**
- Missing, incomplete, or patently insufficient requirements
- Submitted total bid price exceeds the ABC

Only bids that are determined to contain all bid requirements for both components shall be rated "passed."

### 59.3 MEARB Evaluation
For Goods and Infrastructure Projects using MEARB, detailed implementation is provided in Sections 61.3 to 61.8.

### 59.4 Consulting Services
Detailed implementation is provided in Section 62.

---

## Section 60. Ceiling for Bid Prices

### 60.1 Upper Limit
The ABC shall be the upper limit or ceiling for acceptable bid prices. There shall be no lower limit or floor on the amount of the award.

### 60.2 Foreign-Funded Procurement Conditions
The ABC shall be applied as the ceiling, provided:
- a) Bidding Documents are obtainable free of charge online
- b) ABC is based on recent estimates and prevailing market prices
- c) Procuring Entity has trained cost estimators
- d) A system to monitor and report bid prices relative to ABC is established
- e) A monitoring and evaluation system for contract implementation is established

## Section 61. Bid Evaluation for the Procurement of Goods and Infrastructure Projects

### 61.1 Communication Prohibition
Members of the BAC, staff, personnel, Secretariat, TWG, and Observers are prohibited from making or accepting any communication with any bidder regarding the evaluation of their bids until the issuance of the Notice of Award.

**Exception:** The BAC may ask for clarification in writing.

### 61.2 LCB Evaluation Steps

#### 61.2.1 Detailed Evaluation Criteria
- a) **Completeness of the bid** - All required items must be addressed
  - Zero (0) or dash (-) prices are construed as offered for free
- b) **Arithmetical corrections** - Computational errors and omissions

#### 61.2.2 Equal Footing Evaluation
All bidders shall include the cost of all taxes (VAT, income tax, local taxes, etc.) in the bid form.

#### 61.2.3 Discrepancy Resolution
| Discrepancy | Prevailing Value |
|-------------|-----------------|
| Bid prices in figures vs. words | Words |
| Total price per item vs. unit price × quantity | Unit price × quantity |
| Stated total price vs. actual sum | Actual sum |
| Unit cost in detailed estimate vs. bill of quantities | Bill of quantities |
| Amount in price schedule vs. bid form | Bid form |

#### 61.2.4 Ranking
Bids ranked in ascending order of total calculated bid prices. Prices exceeding ABC shall be disqualified.

#### 61.2.5 Timeline
Entire evaluation process shall be completed within **seven (7) calendar days** from the deadline for receipt of proposals.

### 61.3 MEAB Evaluation Steps

- a) Quality proposal evaluated first using criteria in Section 61.4
- b) Price proposals of bidders meeting minimum quality score are then opened
- c) **Weight allocation:**
  - Price proposal: minimum **15%** to maximum **40%**
  - Quality criteria: adjusted to equal 100% total
- d) Bidder with best overall quality-price score is the MEAB
- e) HoPE shall approve or disapprove within **two (2) calendar days**

### 61.4 Quality Component Criteria

Quality assessment may include:
- a) Quality and technical merit, including track record
- b) Aesthetic and functional design
- c) Approach and methodology
- d) Accessibility
- e) Tools and equipment
- f) Social, environmental, economic, and innovative characteristics
- g) Organization, qualification, and experience of assigned personnel
- h) Ongoing contracts and work commitments
- i) After-sales service and technical assistance
- j) Delivery conditions
- k) Disposal measures
- l) Other relevant criteria

### 61.5 Bias Elimination
It is recommended that the highest and lowest scores for each bidder for each criterion shall not be considered, except when evaluation is conducted collegially.

### 61.6 Results Posting
All participating bidders shall be furnished with the results of the evaluation (ranking and total scores only) after HoPE approval. Results shall also be posted on PhilGEPS and Procuring Entity website for at least **seven (7) calendar days**.

### 61.7 MEARB Timeline
Entire evaluation process shall be completed within **fourteen (14) calendar days** from the deadline for receipt of proposals.

### 61.8 MARB Evaluation
- Quality proposals evaluated using quality components in Section 61.4
- Bidder with highest technical rating's financial proposal is opened
- If financial proposal is equal to or lower than ABC, bid is accepted as MAB
- Otherwise, bid is rejected and bidder is disqualified

## Section 62. Bid Evaluation of Short-Listed Bidders for Consulting Services

### 62.1 Communication Prohibition
Same as Section 61.1 for Goods and Infrastructure Projects.

### 62.2 Evaluation Procedures

#### 62.2.1.1 Quality-Based Evaluation Procedure

- a) Two-stage procedure with technical and financial proposals in separate sealed envelopes
- b) Technical proposals opened first, evaluated, and ranked
- c) HoPE approves or disapproves within **two (2) calendar days**
- d) Financial proposal of Highest Rated Bid opened within **three (3) calendar days** after HoPE approval
- e) Negotiations conducted per Section 62.2.5

#### 62.2.1.2 Quality-Cost Based Evaluation Procedure

- a) Technical and financial proposals considered together
- b) Technical proposals evaluated first; financial proposals of those meeting minimum score are opened
- c) **Weight allocation:**
  - Financial proposal: minimum **15%** to maximum **40%**
  - Technical criteria: adjusted to equal 100% total
- d) HoPE approves or disapproves within **two (2) calendar days**
- e) Negotiations conducted per Section 62.2.5 (except for financial proposal)

### 62.2.2 Technical Proposal Evaluation Criteria

- a) **Quality of personnel:**
  - Suitability of key staff
  - General qualifications and competence
  - Education and training
- b) **Experience and capability:**
  - Records of previous engagement
  - Quality of performance
  - Relationship with clients
  - Work commitments
- c) **Plan of approach and methodology:**
  - Clarity and feasibility
  - Innovativeness and comprehensiveness
  - Quality of interpretation of project problems

**For complex undertakings:** Oral presentations may be required within **fifteen (15) calendar days** after the deadline for submission.

### 62.2.3 Bias Elimination
Same recommendation as Section 61.5.

### 62.2.4 Results Posting
Same as Section 61.6.

### 62.2.5 Negotiations

Negotiations shall cover:
- a) Discussion and clarification of terms of reference and scope
- b) Discussion and finalization of methodology and work program
- c) Consideration of qualifications and compensation of personnel
- d) Discussion of services, facilities, and data to be provided
- e) Discussion of financial proposal submitted
- f) Provisions of the contract

**Timeline:** Negotiations shall be completed within **ten (10) calendar days**.

### 62.2.6 Price Ceiling
Calculated bid prices exceeding ABC shall not be considered.

### 62.3 Key Personnel Replacement
No replacement of key personnel before contract award, except for:
- Delay caused by Procuring Entity
- Justifiable reasons (illness, death, resignation) with relevant certificates

### 62.4 Evaluation Timeline
Entire evaluation process shall be completed in not more than **twenty-one (21) calendar days** after the deadline for receipt of proposals.
