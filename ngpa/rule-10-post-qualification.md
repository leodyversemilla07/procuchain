# Rule X - Post-Qualification

## Sections 63-65

This rule covers the post-qualification process, failure of bidding, and single responsive bid submission.

---

## Section 63. Objective and Process of Post-Qualification

### 63.1 Objective
The Bidder with the LCB, MAB, MEAB (for Goods and Infrastructure), or HRB (for Consulting Services) shall undergo post-qualification to determine whether the bidder complies with and is responsive to all requirements and conditions.

### 63.2 Document Submission
Within **five (5) calendar days** from receipt of the BAC notice, the bidder shall submit:
- All eligibility documents supporting its PhilGEPS Certificate of Registration (Platinum Membership)
- Latest income and business tax returns filed for the preceding quarter (not earlier than two quarters from bid submission date)
- Other appropriate licenses and permits required by law

**Failure Consequences:**
- Failure to submit on time or finding against veracity shall disqualify the bidder for award
- Finding against veracity shall cause forfeiture of the Bid Security

### 63.3 Verification and Validation
The BAC shall verify and validate documents using non-discretionary criteria:

**a) Legal Requirements:**
- Verify licenses, certificates, permits, and agreements
- Confirm not included in any "blacklist"
- GPPB shall maintain a portal containing consolidated blacklisted entities

**b) Technical Requirements:**
- Verify and validate stated compliance with Bidding Document specifications

**c) Financial Requirements:**
- Verify and validate bid price proposal
- Verify committed Line of Credit or NFCC

### 63.4 Successful Post-Qualification
If the bidder passes all criteria:
- BAC shall declare the bid as LCRB, MEARB, MARB (Goods/Infrastructure) or HRRB (Consulting Services)
- BAC shall recommend award to HoPE at:
  - Submitted bid price or calculated bid price (whichever is lower)
  - For quality-based evaluation: submitted bid price or negotiated price (whichever is lower)

### 63.5 Failed Post-Qualification
If the bidder fails the criteria:
- BAC shall immediately notify the bidder in writing
- Notification shall include grounds for post-disqualification

### 63.6 Second Bidder Post-Qualification
Immediately after notifying the first bidder of post-disqualification:
- BAC shall initiate post-qualification of the bidder with the second LCB, MEAB, MAB, or HRB
- If second bidder passes and first bidder's reconsideration is denied, second bidder is post-qualified

### 63.7 Subsequent Post-Qualification
If the second bidder fails:
- Procedure is repeated for the bidder with next LCB, MEAB, MAB, or HRB
- Process continues until LCRB, MEARB, MARB, or HRRB is determined for award

### 63.8 Post-Qualification Timeline

| Category | Maximum Period |
|----------|----------------|
| Goods & Infrastructure | 12 calendar days from determination of LCB, MEAB, or MAB |
| Consulting Services | 12 calendar days from completion of negotiation with HRB |

**Extension:** HoPE may extend in exceptional cases, but aggregate period shall not exceed:
- **45 calendar days** for Goods and Infrastructure Projects
- **30 calendar days** for Consulting Services

In case of post-disqualification, the BAC shall be given the same fresh period for the next bidder.

## Section 64. Failure of Bidding

### 64.1 Grounds for Failure
There shall be a failure of bidding in competitive bidding, limited source bidding, or competitive dialogue, if:

- a) No bids are received
- b) All bidders are declared ineligible
- c) No bid qualifies as the SCRB, MARB, LCRB, HRRB, or MEARB
- d) Bidder with the qualifying bid refuses, without justifiable cause, to accept the award of contract

**For other modes:** The BAC may declare a failure if negotiations fail or any of the above instances occur.

### 64.2 Mandatory Review
To determine the reason for failed bidding, the End-User or Implementing Unit shall conduct a mandatory review and evaluation of:
- Terms, conditions, and specifications in the Bidding Documents
- Cost estimates

### 64.3 Revision and Re-bidding
Based on findings:
- BAC shall revise terms, conditions, and specifications
- If necessary, adjust ABC (subject to required approvals)
- Conduct a re-bidding with re-publishing (per Section 50)

### 64.4 Eligible Bidders
All bidders who have initially responded to the Invitation to Bid or Request for Expression of Interest and have been declared eligible or short-listed in the previous biddings shall be allowed to submit new bids.

### 64.5 Second Failure of Bidding
Should a second failure of bidding occur, the Procuring Entity may resort to **negotiated procurement** as provided in Section 35.

---

## Section 65. Single Calculated/Rated/Economically Advantageous and Responsive Bid Submission

The Single Calculated Responsive Bid (SCRB), Single Rated Responsive Bid (SRRB), Single Economically Advantageous Responsive Bid (SEARB), or Single Advantageous Responsive Bid (SARB) shall be considered for award if:

- a) Only one prospective bidder applies for eligibility check, meets the requirements, and submits a responsive bid
- b) More than one prospective bidder applies, but only one meets eligibility requirements and submits a responsive bid
- c) More than one bidder meets eligibility requirements, but only one submits a bid that is responsive

**Important:** In all instances, the Procuring Entity shall ensure that the ABC reflects the most advantageous prevailing price for the Government.
