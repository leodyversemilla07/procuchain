## Key Principles (Section 3)

The NGPA is governed by the following principles:
1. **Transparency** - Open contracting practices and disclosure
2. **Competitiveness** - Equal opportunity for all eligible bidders
3. **Efficiency** - Streamlined processes with emerging technologies
4. **Proportionality** - Requirements proportional to project needs
5. **Accountability** - Responsibility for actions and decisions
6. **Participatory Procurement** - Public monitoring
7. **Sustainability** - Whole life cycle consideration
8. **Professionalism** - Certification and competency frameworks
