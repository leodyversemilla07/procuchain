# Rule XVII - Settlement of Disputes

## Section 88

This rule covers the settlement of disputes arising from procurement contracts.

---

## Section 88. Settlement of Disputes

### 88.1 Amicable Settlement
The parties to a contract shall exert all reasonable efforts to **amicably settle** any dispute that may arise during the implementation of the contract.

### 88.2 Timeline for Amicable Settlement
Amicable settlement shall be attempted within a period of **thirty (30) calendar days** from the time the dispute arises.

### 88.3 Arbitration
If amicable settlement fails, disputes shall be submitted to **arbitration** in accordance with:
- Republic Act No. 9285 (Alternative Dispute Resolution Act of 2004)
- The rules and regulations of the arbitral body
- The provisions of the contract

### 88.4 Arbitral Body
The parties may agree on an arbitral body, which may include:
- The Construction Industry Arbitration Commission (CIAC) for construction contracts
- Other recognized arbitration bodies

### 88.5 Jurisdiction of Courts
**The Regional Trial Courts** shall have original and exclusive jurisdiction over disputes arising from the implementation of government contracts subject to the following:

- a) Resort to arbitration as provided above must be exhausted
- b) Court intervention is limited to:
  - Cases involving criminal liability
  - Cases where the amount in controversy is below the jurisdictional amount for arbitration
  - Cases where both parties agree to court jurisdiction

### 88.6 Continuation of Contract Performance
**Pending resolution of disputes:**
- The Contractor shall continue performance of the contract
- The Procuring Entity shall continue to pay for work accomplished
- Unless otherwise directed by the HoPE

### 88.7 Costs of Settlement
The costs of arbitration or court proceedings shall be:
- Borne by the losing party
- Or shared as determined by the arbitral body or court
