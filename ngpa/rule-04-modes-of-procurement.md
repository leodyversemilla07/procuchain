# Rule IV - Modes of Procurement

## Sections 26-40

This rule covers all the different modes of procurement available under the New Government Procurement Act.

---

## Section 26. Modes of Procurement

### 26.1 Available Modes
The Procuring Entity shall adopt any of the following modes of procurement consistent with the Fit-for-Purpose procurement approach:

- a) Competitive Bidding
- b) Limited Source Bidding
- c) Competitive Dialogue
- d) Unsolicited Offer with Bid Matching
- e) Direct Contracting
- f) Direct Acquisition
- g) Repeat Order
- h) Small Value Procurement
- i) Negotiated Procurement
- j) Direct Sales
- k) Direct Procurement for Science, Technology and Innovation

### 26.2 Detailed Procedures
The detailed procedure for each mode of procurement shall be provided in the manual to be issued by the GPPB.

### 26.3 GPPB Authority
The GPPB is authorized to approve changes in the procurement process to adapt to improvements in modern and emerging technologies.

### 26.4 Delegation
Except for Competitive Bidding, Limited Source Bidding, Competitive Dialogue, and Unsolicited Offer with Bid Matching, the BAC may delegate the conduct of procurement activities for the other modes of procurement to the End-User or Implementing Unit, or the Procurement Unit of the Procuring Entity.

---

## Section 27. Competitive Bidding

### 27.1 Definition and Processes
Competitive Bidding is a mode of procurement which is open to participation by any eligible bidder, and which consists of the following processes:

- a) Publication
- b) Pre-bid conference
- c) Eligibility screening of prospective bidders
- d) Receipt and opening of bids
- e) Evaluation of bids
- f) Post-qualification
- g) Award of contract

Competitive Bidding shall be subject to the provisions of Rules VI to XI of this IRR.

---

## Section 28. Limited Source Bidding

### 28.1 Definition
Limited Source Bidding is a mode of procurement that involves direct invitation to bid by the Procuring Entity to a set of pre-selected suppliers, contractors, or consultants with known experience and proven capability relative to the requirements of a particular contract.

### 28.2 Conditions for Use
Limited Source Bidding may only be resorted to under any of the following conditions:

- a) Procurement of highly specialized or complex types of Goods, and Consulting Services which are known to be obtainable only from a limited number of sources.
- b) Procurement of major plant components where it is deemed advantageous to limit the bidding to known eligible bidders in order to maintain an optimum and uniform level of quality and performance of the plant as a whole.
- c) Procurement of highly specialized infrastructure projects involving and affecting national security.

### 28.3 Pre-selected List
The pre-selected suppliers, contractors, or consultants shall be those appearing on a list maintained by the relevant government authority that has expertise in the type of procurement concerned.

### 28.4 Pre-selection Criteria
The pre-selection shall be based on the following characteristics:

- a) Capability and resources of the bidders to perform the contract
- b) Capabilities with respect to personnel equipment or manufacturing facilities
- c) Financial position
- d) Good standing with respect to its affiliation

### 28.5 Procedure
Except for the publication requirement under Section 50, the Procuring Entity shall observe the procedure for Competitive Bidding and may adopt reduced timelines within the total maximum period prescribed in this IRR.

---

## Section 29. Competitive Dialogue

### 29.1 Definition
Competitive Dialogue is a mode of procurement that employs a two-stage bidding process where the Procuring Entity invites suppliers, manufacturers, distributors, contractors, consultants, and service providers to a dialogue to propose solutions to address its needs and requirements.

### 29.2 Conditions for Use
Competitive Dialogue may be resorted to if any of the following conditions are present:

- a) The procurement includes innovative design or solutions involving procurement of complex purchases
- b) The contract requires prior negotiations with the suppliers due to specific circumstances related to nature, complexity, legal and financial issues, or risks attached to the procurement
- c) The Technical Specifications cannot be sufficiently established or precisely defined by the Procuring Entity

### 29.3 Publication Requirements
The Procuring Entity shall provide the preliminary technical specifications of the project with minimum requirements in the Invitation to Bid or Request for Expression of Interest.

### 29.4 Two-Stage Process

#### 29.4.1 First Stage
- Eligibility requirements and initial technical proposals are evaluated
- Dialogue conducted to determine, identify, describe, define, and finalize technical components
- Maximum periods for submission:
  - Goods: 45 calendar days
  - Infrastructure Projects: 65 calendar days
  - Consulting Services: 75 calendar days

#### 29.4.2 Second Stage
- Final technical and financial proposals are submitted and evaluated
- Contract awarded using LCRB, MEARB, MARB or HRRB criteria

---

## Section 30. Unsolicited Offer with Bid Matching

### 30.1 Definition
Unsolicited Offer with Bid Matching is a mode of procurement wherein the Procuring Entity may consider unsolicited offers from the original offeror on a negotiated basis for Goods and Consulting Services.

### 30.2 Requirements for Unsolicited Offer
The manufacturer, distributor, supplier, or consultant shall submit:

- a) Details of the new concept or technology
- b) Proof of concept
- c) Legal, technical, and financial eligibility documents
- d) Proposed terms and conditions

### 30.3 Evaluation Timeline
Within 20 calendar days from receipt, the Procuring Entity shall assess whether the project being offered is necessary.

### 30.4 First in Time Approach
In case of multiple similar unsolicited offers, the first in time, stronger in right approach shall be applied.

### 30.5-30.8 Evaluation and Award Process
- Evaluation completed within 60 calendar days
- Negotiation completed within 15 calendar days
- Publication of Invitation to Bid within 30 calendar days from conferment of original offeror status
- Bid matching period of 15 calendar days

---

## Section 31. Direct Contracting

### 31.1 Definition
Direct Contracting is a mode of procurement of Goods that does not require elaborate Bidding Documents.

### 31.2 Conditions
Direct contracting may only be resorted to in any of the following conditions:

- a) When the goods are proprietary in nature
- b) When the procurement of critical components from a specific manufacturer is a condition precedent to guarantee project performance
- c) When sold by an exclusive dealer or manufacturer

### 31.3 Procedure
- a) BAC prepares Request for Quotation (RFQ)
- b) Simplified negotiations may be conducted
- c) BAC recommends award of contract

---

## Section 32. Direct Acquisition

### 32.1 Definition
Direct Acquisition is a mode of procurement of CSE not available in the PS-DBM, Non-CSE, and services with ABC not exceeding **Two Hundred Thousand Pesos (₱200,000.00)**.

### 32.2 APP Requirement
Procurement through Direct Acquisition shall be reflected in the APP.

### 32.3 Authorization
The HoPE may authorize the End-User or Implementing Unit to directly purchase from any known and reputable source.

---

## Section 33. Repeat Order

### 33.1 Definition
Repeat Order is a mode of procurement wherein the Procuring Entity may procure goods from the previous winning bidder whenever there arises a need to replenish goods procured under a contract previously awarded through bidding.

### 33.2 Conditions
- a) Unit price must be equal to or lower than original contract and prevailing market price
- b) Must be availed within **six (6) months** from date of NTP
- c) Shall not exceed **25%** of the quantity of each item in the original contract

---

## Section 34. Small Value Procurement

### 34.1 Definition and Threshold
Small Value Procurement (SVP) is a mode of procurement whereby the Procuring Entity requests for the submission of at least three (3) price quotations.

The receipt of one (1) quotation is sufficient to proceed with the evaluation, provided the amount does not exceed **Two Million Pesos (₱2,000,000.00)**.

### 34.2 LGU Thresholds

| LGU Classification | Province | City | Municipality |
|-------------------|----------|------|--------------|
| 1st Class | ₱2,000,000 | ₱2,000,000 | ₱400,000 |
| 2nd Class | ₱2,000,000 | ₱2,000,000 | ₱400,000 |
| 3rd Class | ₱2,000,000 | ₱1,600,000 | ₱400,000 |
| 4th Class | ₱1,600,000 | ₱1,200,000 | ₱200,000 |
| 5th Class | ₱1,200,000 | ₱800,000 | ₱200,000 |

**For Barangays: ₱100,000**

### 34.3 Procedure
- a) End-User submits request with Technical Specifications, ABC, and other terms
- b) RFQ/RFP posted for 3 calendar days (except for ABC ≤₱200,000)
- c) BAC sends RFQs to at least 3 suppliers
- d) Pre-bid conference may be conducted at BAC's discretion
- e) Abstract of Quotations prepared after deadline
- f) Award to supplier with LCRB, MEARB, MARB or HRRB

---

## Section 35. Negotiated Procurement

Negotiated Procurement is a mode of procurement of Goods, Infrastructure Projects, and Consulting services, whereby the Procuring Entity directly negotiates a contract with a technically, legally, and financially capable supplier, contractor or consultant.

### 35.1 Two Failed Biddings
Where there have been two (2) failures of bidding. ABC cannot be increased by more than 20% of the ABC for the last failed bidding.

### 35.2 Emergency Cases
- a) Imminent danger to life or property during a state of calamity
- b) When time is of the essence arising from natural or man-made calamities
- c) Other causes where immediate action is necessary

### 35.3 Take-over of Contracts
For contracts rescinded or terminated where immediate action is necessary.

### 35.4 Adjacent or Contiguous
For contracts adjacent or contiguous to ongoing Infrastructure Projects or Consulting Services.

### 35.5 Agency-to-Agency
Procurement from another agency of the government which has the mandate and absorptive capacity.

### 35.6 Scientific, Scholarly or Artistic Work
For work of art, restorative works, scientific/academic work, highly specialized medical equipment, etc.

### 35.7 Highly Technical Consultants
For individual consultants hired to do highly technical or primarily confidential work.

### 35.8 Defense Cooperation Agreements
For DND procurement involving major defense equipment.

### 35.9 Lease of Real Property and Venue
For official use of government agencies.

### 35.10 NGO Participation
When appropriation law earmarks amount to be specifically contracted out to NGOs.

### 35.11 Community Participation
Direct purchase from local farmers, fisherfolk, and community/social groups.

### 35.12 UN Agencies
Direct procurement from specialized UN agencies, international organizations or international financing institutions.

### 35.13 Direct Retail Purchase of POL Products, Electronic Charging Devices, and Online Subscriptions
For petroleum fuel, oil and lubricant products, electronic charging devices, and online subscriptions.

## Section 36. Direct Sales

### 36.1 Definition
Direct Sales is a mode of procurement of Non-CSE where a Procuring Entity directly purchases from a supplier that has satisfactorily delivered Non-CSE to another government agency under a completed contract.

### 36.2 Conditions
- a) Procurement project included in approved APP
- b) Original Non-CSE procured through Competitive Bidding, Limited Source Bidding, Competitive Dialogue or Unsolicited Offer with Bid Matching
- c) Certification of satisfactory performance issued
- d) Procurement initiated within **six (6) months** from acceptance
- e) Contract within original contract amount and same or lower unit price
- f) Supplier remains legally, technically, and financially capable

---

## Section 37. Direct Procurement for Science, Technology, and Innovation

### 37.1 Definition
Direct Procurement for Science, Technology and Innovation is a mode of procurement for:

- a) Supplies, materials, equipment for research and development
- b) Goods which include products of a commissioned task
- c) Other analogous goods

---

## Section 38. Video Recording Requirements

### 38.1 Recording Requirement
The Procuring Entity shall keep a video recording of all procurement-related conferences for Competitive Bidding.

### 38.2 Livestreaming
The Procuring Entity shall livestream the preliminary examination and opening of bids.

### 38.3 Thresholds for Mandatory Recording
- Goods: Above ₱10,000,000
- Infrastructure Projects: Above ₱20,000,000
- Consulting Services: Above ₱5,000,000

### 38.4 Storage Period
Copies shall be stored for not less than **five (5) years**.

---

## Section 39. Prohibition on Splitting of Government Contracts

### 39.1 Definition
Splitting of Government Contracts involves dividing or breaking up of contracts into smaller quantities and amounts for the purpose of evading requirements.

### 39.2 Exception for Infrastructure Projects
Projects may be implemented by phases with clear delineation of work.

---

## Section 40. Procurement of CSE

### 40.1 Mandatory Procurement from PS-DBM
All NGAs, GOCCs, GFIs, SUCs, and LGUs shall procure CSE only from the PS-DBM, subject to its availability.

### 40.2 Exceptions
- a) CSE unavailable due to zero inventory
- b) Technical specifications not sufficient
- c) Available stock not sufficient
- d) BAC resolves procurement from other sources is for efficiency, practicality, or economic viability

### 40.3 PS-DBM Direct Dealing
PS-DBM may deal directly with manufacturers or exclusive distributors.

### 40.4 Consignment
Arrangement whereby the consignor gives possession of goods to the consignee without transfer of ownership.

### 40.5 CSE Classification
PS-DBM shall develop parameters for identification, expansion, and definition of CSE requirements.
