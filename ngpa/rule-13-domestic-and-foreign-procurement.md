# Rule XIII - Domestic and Foreign Procurement

## Sections 78-79

This rule covers procurement of domestic and foreign goods and domestic preference provisions.

---

## Section 78. Procurement of Domestic and Foreign Goods

Consistent with the country's obligations under treaty or international or executive agreement:
- Goods may be obtained from domestic or foreign sources
- Procurement shall be open to all eligible suppliers, manufacturers, and distributors

---

## Section 79. Domestic Preference

### 79.1 Priority for Philippine Products
The Procuring Entity shall give priority and preference to Philippine products and services at all levels of the procurement process, including:
- Raw materials
- Ingredients
- Supplies
- Fixtures

### 79.2 Mandatory Award to Domestic Bidder (Tatak Pinoy Period)
For a period of **ten (10) years** from the effectivity of RA No. 11981 (Tatak Pinoy/Proudly Filipino Act), and for Philippine products and services in sectors covered by the prevailing Tatak Pinoy Strategy:

**The Procuring Entity is mandated to award the contract to the domestic bidder.**

**Domestic Bidder Defined:**
Any person or entity offering:
- Unmanufactured articles, materials, or supplies wholly obtained or produced in the Philippines
- Manufactured articles, materials, or supplies manufactured or to be manufactured in the Philippines substantially from articles, materials, or supplies of Philippine growth, production, or manufacture

### 79.3 Post-Tatak Pinoy Period
After the ten (10) year period counted from the effectivity of RA No. 11981:
- Domestic preference shall be subject to a margin of preference
- Margin shall not be lower than **fifteen percent (15%)**
- Margin determined by the Tatak Pinoy Council

### 79.4 Tatak Pinoy Strategy Governance
For products and services governed by the TPS, domestic preference shall be in accordance with the IRR or guidelines issued for the Tatak Pinoy Act.

### 79.5 Margin of Preference
The Procuring Entity shall award to the domestic bidder if the bid is not more than **twenty-five percent (25%)** in excess of the lowest foreign bid.

**Review:** This margin shall be subject to periodic review and adjustment by the GPPB.

### 79.6 Certification Requirement
A Domestic Bidder can only claim preference if it secures from the appropriate agency a certification that the articles forming part of its bid are substantially composed of articles, materials, or supplies grown, produced, or manufactured in the Philippines.

### 79.7 Waiver Conditions
The domestic preference may be waived if:

- a) Domestic production is insufficient or unavailable in the required commercial quantities
- b) The specific or desired quality is not met
- c) Domestic preference will result in inconsistencies with the Philippines' obligations under treaty or international or executive agreements
- d) Other analogous circumstances
